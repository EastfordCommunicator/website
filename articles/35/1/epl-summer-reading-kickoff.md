---
title: Library Kicks off 'Colonial Days' Summer Reading Program
---

EASTFORD --- On Tuesday, July 7, the Eastford Public Library welcomed
Revolutionary War reenactor Tom Angels, from History Without Walls, to
kick off the library's "Colonial Days Summer Reading Program - Week One:
Colonial Life." Angels, an amateur vexillologist (an expert or scholar
who studies the history, symbolism, and usage of flags), gave attendees
a lesson on Revolutionary War-era flags and their symbolism. He also
walked people through the various types of clothing colonists would have
worn. Children between the ages of 4-7 left behind their simple frocks
and began dressing just as the adults in their lives! Young volunteers
remarked on the heaviness of the many layers of clothing.
