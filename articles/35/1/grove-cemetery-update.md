---
title: From the Grove Cemetery Association Board of Trustees
---

Kindly consider assisting the Eastford Grove Cemetery Association in its
pursuit of efficient perpetual care of the Grove Cemetery. We are a
non-profit group that does not receive funding from the Town of
Eastford. A tax-deductible donation from you will help us meet present
and future beautification and maintenance requirements. Contributions of
any amount will help us continue to meet our goal of providing a
well-maintained, respectable final resting place for your loved ones.

Additionally, you could leave a gift to the Eastford Grove Cemetery
Association in your will or living trust, known as a bequest. This
allows you to offer future support without giving up assets today. Or,
you may also wish to consider making "The Grove Cemetery Association"
the recipient of a memorial donation ("in lieu of flowers").

We appreciate your consideration of our appeal and welcome any questions
or concerns regarding the cemetery operation. Questions? Call (860)
428-0280. [You may also visit our website here](https://sites.google.com/site/eastfordgrovecemetery/).
