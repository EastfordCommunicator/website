---
title: From the Superintendent
author: Donna P. Leake, Ph.D.
---

I hope everyone in our school community is enjoying the summer months
and taking time to relax, reconnect, and create lasting memories with
family and friends. While students are enjoying a well-deserved break,
summer remains one of our busiest seasons as we prepare for another
successful school year.

- Summer Preparations for the New School Year: The district and school
administrative teams are focused on implementing procedures and
protocols that will ensure a welcoming and successful return for the
Eastford Elementary School staff on Aug. 25 and students on Aug. 27.

Our custodial team has been hard at work cleaning, painting, and making
repairs throughout the school. Small groups of teachers have been
working collaboratively to refine curriculum and assessment practices to
provide every student the opportunity to learn and succeed. Once their
classrooms are cleaned, teachers will begin setting up their learning
spaces and preparing an engaging, welcoming environment for students.

- Summer Facilities Improvements: Several facility improvement projects
are underway to enhance our learning environment and make our school
ready to welcome students this fall. This summer, our gymnasium is
receiving a fresh coat of paint, and the middle school roof is being
replaced. Modifications have also been made to several lower-level
classroom spaces to better support a variety of student needs.
Additional structural improvements have been completed on both the
inside and outside of the connecting corridor leading to the playground
and storage areas. These important investments reflect our continued
commitment to providing a safe, welcoming, and supportive learning
environment for all students.

- Transportation and After-School Programs: EASTCONN will once again
provide school bus transportation for our students. Once finalized,
updated bus routes will be posted on our district website. Since we will
be utilizing three buses to transport students to and from school, we
anticipate shorter routes, which should allow for later morning pickup
times and earlier afternoon arrival times at home.

The Hale YMCA will once again offer before- and after-school programs
for our students. Thanks to the continuation of our after-school grant,
we will be able to offset the cost of after-school care for students in
grades K--8. Additional details are available in the flyer posted on our
website.

In addition to YMCA programming, we will also offer free extended-day
learning opportunities for students in grades K--8.

- Back-to-School Information: All families with students attending
Eastford Elementary School will receive a letter from Principal Carole
McCombe containing important information about the start of the school
year.

If you are new to Eastford, please contact the Eastford Elementary
School office as soon as possible to register your children in grades
pre-K--12. Information about the registration process, the 2026--2027
school calendar, district and school policies, and other important
updates is available on our website at www.eastfordct.org. Please note
that information for Eastford high school students is best accessed
through their respective high school websites.

- Staffing Update: Please join me in congratulating Mr. Charlie Kernan,
our director of Pupil Services and middle school Social Studies teacher,
on his appointment as Assistant PPS Director in the Killingly School
District.

Mr. Kernan has made a significant impact on our district, serving in
multiple roles with dedication, compassion, and an unwavering commitment
to students. While we will certainly miss him, we are proud of this
well-deserved opportunity and grateful for his years of outstanding
service to our school community.

As is often the case in a small district, Mr. Kernan fulfilled many
important responsibilities. We are actively seeking qualified candidates
to continue this important work. We are also in the process of hiring an
additional special education teacher to strengthen our continuum of
educational services and expand programming options for students. We
remain committed to recruiting talented educators who will become valued
members of our school community.

- Designated High School Study: At its summer meeting on Thursday, Aug.
20, at 6 p.m., the Board of Education will receive a report from the Ad
Hoc High School Committee regarding the selection of the district's
designated high school. Over the past several weeks, the committee has
carefully reviewed options and gathered information to help guide this
important decision.

Community input will be an essential part of the process. The Board of
Education will host a Community Conversation on Thursday, Sept. 24, from
6-8 p.m. Residents are encouraged to attend, learn more about the
committee's work, ask questions, and share their perspectives before the
board considers its next steps. The invitation to this Community
Conversation is posted on the Eastford School website.

Our district's success is built upon the support and involvement of this
wonderful community, and I encourage everyone to participate in this
important conversation about our future.

Looking Ahead

As we look ahead, we remain committed to academic excellence, student
well-being, school safety, and strong partnerships with our families and
community. I look forward to welcoming everyone back for another year of
learning, growth, and collaboration.

In the meantime, I wish all members of the Eastford community continued
enjoyment of the summer. If you have any questions, concerns, or would
like to schedule an appointment, please feel free to contact me at
<dleake@eastfordct.org>. You are also always welcome to stop by and say
hello before one of our regularly scheduled Board of Education meetings.
