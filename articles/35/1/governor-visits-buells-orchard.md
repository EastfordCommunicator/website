---
title: Lamont, Agriculture Officials Visit Buell's Orchard
---

EASTFORD --- On Friday, July 17, Buell's Orchard played host to Gov. Ned
Lamont and Commissioner of Agriculture Bryan Hurlburt. Officials
discussed the state of specialty crop agriculture, especially fruit
growing in Connecticut. Owners Jeff and Jonathan Sandness were also
joined by State Sen. Jeff Gordon, CT Farm Bureau President Paul Larson
and Eastford First Selectman Deb Richards.
