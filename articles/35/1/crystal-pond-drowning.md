---
title: Community Rallies around Local Family After Teenager Dies in Eastford
author: Adam Minor
---

EASTFORD --- A 15-year-old teenager from East Hampton has passed away
after drowning in Crystal Pond in Eastford on Sunday, July 12, after
experiencing a medical emergency while she was paddleboarding.

According to information provided by the Connecticut Department of
Energy and Environmental Protection (DEEP), the Connecticut
Environmental Conservation (EnCon) Police responded to Crystal Pond in
Eastford on Sunday, July 12, at 12:15 p.m., in response to a report of a
person missing from a paddleboard.

Witnesses reported that the victim appeared to have a medical emergency
and fell into the water. The victim, who was later identified as
Cassandra Munson, was recovered by a good Samaritan who came out onto
the pond to assist. The victim received treatment onshore and was
transported to Day Kimball Hospital, where she was pronounced deceased.

The Office of the Chief Medical Examiner will determine the cause and
manner of death. The incident is under investigation by EnCon Police.

In the days following the incident, Cassandra's aunt, Lisa Richards,
established a GoFundMe to help pay for "funeral, memorial, and other
expenses associated with honoring Cassandra and supporting her immediate
family."

"As anyone can understand, our hearts are completely shattered, and our
lives will never be the same," Richards wrote on the GoFundMe page. "No
parent should ever have to bury their child. As our family tries to
process this devastating loss, my sister is facing the overwhelming
burden of planning Cassie's funeral while also trying to be present for
her younger daughter, Morgan, who is only 11 years old and is also
grieving the loss of her mentor and older sister."

CT DEEP extended its sympathies to the victim's family and friends, and
reported that no further information will be released at this time. For
the family, the road ahead will be filled with struggle.

"The journey through grief is long," Richards wrote on the GoFundMe
page. "Your generosity will provide them with stability and support as
they begin to rebuild their lives one day at a time. If you are able to
give, no amount is too small. If you are unable to donate, we ask that
you please share this page, keep our family in your thoughts and
prayers, and continue sharing memories of our little girl. From the
bottom of our hearts, thank you for your kindness, compassion, and
generosity during the most difficult time our family has faced. We will
forever cherish the memories of Cassandra's life. She was deeply loved;
she always loved deeply in return, and there will be a void in our
hearts that will never heal."

As of press time, the GoFundMe had accumulated more than 750 donations
totaling nearly $61,000, with the total still rising. [For more
information on the GoFundMe or to support the family, click here](https://www.gofundme.com/f/support-for-cassies-funeral-and-her-grieving-family).
