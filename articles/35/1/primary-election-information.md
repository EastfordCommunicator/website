---
title: Connecticut Primary Election
---
**Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026**

**6 a.m. -- 8 p.m.**

**Town Office Building**

Early Voting: Early voting for the Primary will be conducted at the
lower level of the Town Office Building during the following
dates/times:

Monday, Aug. 3; Wednesday, Aug. 5; Friday, Aug. 7; Saturday, Aug. 8; and
Sunday, Aug. 9. The hours will be from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. On Tuesday,
Aug. 4, and Thursday, Aug. 6, the hours will be extended to 8 a.m. to 8
p.m.

Voter Registration cards are available from the Registrar of Voters and
are available in the Town Clerk's Office.

[Online voter registration through the Secretary of State's Office can be
accessed here](https://voterregistration.ct.gov/OLVR/welcome.do).

[Residents may also verify their voter information, including address and
party affiliation, by visiting here](https://portaldir.ct.gov/sots/lookup.aspx).

Enrollment hours for voter registration are located on the lower level
of the Town Office Building.
