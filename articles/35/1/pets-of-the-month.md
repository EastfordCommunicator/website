title: "Every Pet Has Its Day: Meet Some of Eastford's Cutest Companions"
---

EASTFORD --- Our readers certainly delivered! After inviting the
community to share photos of their cutest pets, the *Eastford
Communicator* was flooded with entries featuring their lovable
companions! While every pet deserves a blue ribbon in the eyes of its
family, we've selected a collection of submissions to celebrate the
companions that bring so much joy to homes across our community. Thank
you to everyone who participated and helped make this one of our most
heartwarming photo features yet!
