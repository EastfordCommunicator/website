---
title: Celebrating America
---

EASTFORD --- Travis Young, of Eastford, sent the *Communicator* this
photo of his house and classic American pickup truck proudly flying Old
Glory in celebration of our nation's 250th birthday. "It's a tribute to
the greatest country in the world, made possible by the brave men and
women who have fought and continue to fight to protect our freedom,"
Young said.
