---
title: Family Fun Takes Center Stage at CCE Frog Jump Event
---

EASTFORD --- On Saturday, July 11, Crystal Pond Park was filled with the
smell of hamburgers and hot dogs, the sounds of music by the East
Woodstock Cornet Band and the squeals of children as they watched frogs
jump. The Congregational Church of Eastford held its annual Frog Jump,
Band Concert and Cookout, continuing a tradition that has endured for
nearly a century. Along with the frog-jumping contest (see results
below), attendees were treated to a full cookout, cotton candy, ice
cream, a bounce house, swimming in Crystal Pond, and the traditional
cakewalk to conclude the evening, which awarded desserts to random
walkers in a musical-chairs style. For more photos of the winners of the
cakewalk portion of the event, [check out the *Eastford Communicato*r
Facebook page!](https://www.facebook.com/people/The-Eastford-Communicator/61557072023031/)
