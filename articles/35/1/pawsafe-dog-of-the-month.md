## Did you Know?

A trained bloodhound's sense of smell is so spot on that it can be
admitted as evidence in a court of law. Perhaps it's because bloodhounds
can follow tracks more than 300 hours old and can stay on a scent trail
for more than 130 miles.

## Dog of the Month

Darcy is a stunning, spotted girl with gorgeous blue eyes and a whole
lot of brains! This smart, active pup loves to learn, explore, and be
part of the action. She would thrive with an active family who can keep
her mind and body busy. Darcy is about 2 years old, weighs 40 pounds, is
dog-friendly, and is ready for her next adventure!
