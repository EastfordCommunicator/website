---
title: What's Going on at the Eastford Public Library?
author: Katie Ball
---

The final days of summer are upon us, and I hope you are soaking them in
with a good book!

Over the next several months, the library will welcome award-winning
writers Paul Trembley, Andrew Clark, and Philip Fracassi. Our
one-of-a-kind Author Talks continue to gain popularity as our Library
Chair, Jody Blanchette, sits in cozy conversation to discuss their
writing, the publishing world, and their life. Her efforts to persuade
authors to visit our small, rural town do not go unnoticed, and we are
grateful to offer this experience to our patrons. To highlight local
talent, the library will also be hosting authors Marie Lisette Rimer and
Sharon Yang. Upcoming dates will be posted shortly. Please inquire at
the front desk if you have any interest in attending.

In the meantime, the library continues to celebrate America250
throughout August by offering programs for all ages. Stop in --- we'd
love to see you.

Library hours are Tuesdays, 11 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.; Wednesdays, 10 a.m. to
5 p.m.; Thursdays, 11 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.; and Saturdays, 9 a.m. to 12
p.m. 

You may reach us at (860) 974-0125, or by e-mail
at <eastfordpublib@gmail.com>. Upcoming events and programs can be seen on
the town page or on social media. If you haven't yet, please
follow [Eastford Public Library on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/Eastford-Public-Library-1004-0125071213114254/) and/or
Instagram [@eastfordpubliclibrary](https://www.instagram.com/eastfordpubliclibrary/).
