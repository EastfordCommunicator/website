title: Legislative Update
author: Sen. Jeff Gordon
---

The state legislature may be out of session, but a lot of work is still
being done, be it attending to constituent services, helping with
municipal matters, being out and about in the towns of the Senate
District, or working on items for next year's session.

I continue to focus on affordability issues, including ways to cut
property taxes by further increasing funding for public education. I
helped get more state funding overall this year. I am working to get
more done. By better funding these costs, we can reduce the pressure on
your property taxes to keep them from rising. It is about making
Connecticut more affordable for you and your family. The state can
afford this without increasing taxes if it refocuses and repurposes the
tax money it already has, and lets you keep your hard-earned money up
front by stopping over-taxing you.

matter, answer a question, or take up a concern. You can call my office
at (860) 240-800, e-mail me at <jeffrey.gordon@cga.ct.gov>, check for news
and sign up for my free e-newsletter at www.senatorgordon.com, or learn
about what I am doing on Facebook at "Senator Jeff Gordon." I'm proud to
represent Eastford!
