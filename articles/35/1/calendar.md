## August

### Monday, Aug. 3

We're going down to the farm! Join us at Eastford Baptist Church
Monday, Aug. 3 through Friday, Aug. 7 for an all-new, EBC original
Vacation Bible School event! Throughout the week, we will be exploring
parables of Jesus Christ! What is the kingdom of God? How does salvation
work? What is our role in spreading the gospel? How important is it to
tell others about Jesus? Learn the answers, and so much more at Down on
the Farm VBS! We will have games, snacks, crafts, contests, giveaways,
and so much more! We will also be hosting a Penny War to benefit a
missionary to be determined! So get your overalls, get on your tractor,
and join us down on the farm this summer for VBS! Please note: All
children signing up must be entering at least Pre-K (at least 3 years of
age) and must be potty trained. If the child is not potty trained, an
adult must remain with the child throughout the week. EBC volunteers are
not permitted to change diapers. [Register today here](https://eastfordbaptist.churchcenter.com/registrations/events/3565861).

### Tuesday, Aug. 4

Eastford Public Library will host "Wool Demo and Weaving," at 3 p.m.
This program will demonstrate how colonial Americans made textiles from
wool. Please register.

### Thursday, Aug. 6

Eastford Public Library will host a special "Story Time" at 11:15 a.m.
with local author Alice Kuzel. Join us as she reads her charming
children's book, "Wooly Forgets," about a forgetful sheep that lives in
Old Sturbridge Village. In the afternoon, we will host a Dungeons &
Dragons club from 4:30-5:30 p.m. This group will continue to meet every
Thursday. Later in the evening, the library will also host "Trivia
Night" at 6:30 p.m. with Jeremy Barlow. Registration for this popular
adult program is a must!

### Tuesday, Aug. 11

Eastford Public Library will host "Revolutionary Connecticut," at 3
p.m., presented by the Connecticut Museum of Culture and History. This
program is for all ages and will explore the roots of revolutionary
sentiment in our state during that time. Please register.

### Wednesday, Aug. 12

Eastford Public Library will host "Craft the Colonial Game Hoops and
Graces," at 3 p.m. In the evening, we welcome teens and adults to take
part in our "Maker's Night" from 6-7:30 p.m. to sew colonial seed bags.
If interested in either program, please register.

Eastford Baptist Church will host its annual Teen Vacation Bible School
nightly from Wednesday, Aug. 12, to Friday, Aug. 14, starting at 6:30
p.m. For teens going into Grades 7-12. Bible study, games, food, and
much more! Come enjoy three nights of fun, food, and faith!

### Thursday, Aug. 13

Eastford Public Library will host "Story Time" at 11 a.m. Join us for a
story, craft, snack, and time of free play.

### Friday, Aug. 14

Want to wrap up the summer with an inexpensive and fun family activity
close to home? We have just the thing for you at Windham-Tolland 4-H
Camp. Family camp starts with check-in on Friday, Aug. 14, at 6:30 p.m.
and ends with a closing circle at 5:30 p.m. on Saturday, Aug. 15, after
dinner. This is a chance to experience a weekend of overnight camping
with the whole family. Bring Mom, Dad, the kids, Grandma, Grandpa,
brothers, sisters, cousins, aunts, and uncles! Or bring your own made-up
family of a group of friends (Note: All children must be accompanied by
an adult). This program started in 2014 with 35 attendees. Last year we
had 81. The word is out that this is a fun event. We often get two and
even three generations in a cabin.

Enjoy an evening campfire on Friday and a full day of camp activities on
Saturday, including swimming, hiking, canoeing, kayaking, sports, low
ropes, art, fishing, and more. Fee is $60 per person and includes
breakfast, lunch, snacks, dinner, lodging in our rustic cabins, and all
activities. Register online at www.4hcampct.org.

### Wednesday, Aug. 19

If you have a child entering Grades 1 through 6, join us at Eastford
Baptist Church from 6-8 p.m. on Wednesday, Aug. 19, for SPLASH! --- a
night of fun water games. SPLASH! is the official kickoff event for EBC
Olympians, a weekly children's ministry that meets every Wednesday
starting in September (this year's Opening Night is Wednesday, Sept. 2)
and going through the first week of June. Come join a community of kids
that want to grow in their faith, learn about the Word of God, and have
fun! For more information, contact EBC Olympians at
<ebcolympians@gmail.com>.
