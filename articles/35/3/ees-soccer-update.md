---
title: Bulldogs begin fall soccer schedule with mixed results
author: Adam Minor
---

EASTFORD — The Eastford Bulldogs’ fall soccer season began last month,
and this year the school has two teams to cheer for, fielding both a
boys’ and a girls’ team for the first time in several years.

Kullen Pacheco is head coach of the boys’ team, while Jen Barlow is head
coach of the girls’ squad. Both teams are now in the midst of a 12-game
schedule in the Quinebaug Valley Junior Conference (QVJC) Small School
division, which this year consists of the following six opponents:
Parish Hill, Canterbury, Thompson, Pomfret, Ashford, and St. James. The
top four teams at the end of the regular season will play a two-round
playoff (Semifinals and Finals) to determine the QVJC Champion. Playoff
schedules will be announced near the end of the regular season.

The boys’ team started the season 2-0, with a 2-1 victory over St. James
on the road on Tuesday, Sept. 15, and then a shutout, 2-0 victory over
Parish Hill at home on Thursday, Sept. 17. Kameron Pacheco scored three
times in the first two games, once against St. James, and twice against
Parish Hill. Jack Kernan added a goal against St. James.

The Bulldogs would falter in the next two games, however, as they lost
at home to Canterbury, 4-1, on Tuesday, Sept. 22, and then were shut
out, 8-0, on the road at Thompson on Thursday, Sept. 24. Charlie
Ellsworth added his first goal of the season against Canterbury.

As of press time Monday, Sept. 28, the boys’ team is 2-2 (1-1 home, 1-1
away) with eight games left on the schedule.

On the girls’ side, the Bulldogs have struggled offensively, losing 6-0
at home to St. James on Tuesday, Sept. 15, then narrowly losing to
Parish Hill, 2-1, on the road to Parish Hill on Thursday, Sept. 17, with
the lone goal of the season thus far being scored by Hayleigh Wooding.
On Tuesday, Sept. 22, the girls were shut out 9-0 on the road at
Canterbury, moving to 0-3 on the season (0-1 home, 0-2 away) as of press
time.

See below for the remaining schedule and season goal leaders, and stay
tuned to the Communicator’s Facebook page for game-by-game coverage!
