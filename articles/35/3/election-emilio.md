---
title: Election Questionnaire for Tony Emilio, House District 50
---

**In your opinion, what are some of the most important issues facing the
50<sup>th</sup> District, and Eastford specifically?**

“For Eastford and the 50th District, the most important issues are
affordability, local control, strong schools, reliable infrastructure,
and public safety. Families, seniors, and small businesses are feeling
the pressure of high taxes, rising energy costs, and the overall cost of
living. I will support responsible state budgeting, oppose unfunded
mandates, and fight to keep more money in Connecticut residents'
pockets. Eastford’s rural character is one of its greatest strengths.
Hartford must respect decisions made by local residents and town
leaders—not impose one-size-fits-all policies. I will advocate for
dependable school funding, safe roads and bridges, expanded rural
broadband, and support for first responders. Most importantly, I will
bring a thoughtful, respectful Republican voice to Hartford — focused on
practical solutions, accountable government, and stronger communities
for every family in our district.”

**How do you hope to tackle these issues if elected to the House of
Representatives?**

“If elected, I will approach these challenges with the same practical,
business-minded discipline I have brought to my work and service in
Pomfret. I will listen first — to families, local officials, educators,
first responders, and small-business owners — then work across the aisle
when it produces real results for our communities. I will fight to
control state spending, reduce unfunded mandates, and protect taxpayers
from policies that drive up property taxes and energy costs. I will
advocate for strong local schools, safe roads and bridges, rural
broadband, and dependable support for public safety. The 50th District
deserves a representative who is accessible, prepared, and focused on
solutions — not partisan talking points. I will bring a respectful
Republican voice to Hartford and work every day to make Connecticut more
affordable, accountable, and better for the families who call our
communities home.”

**Why did you decide to run for election to the House of
Representatives?**

“I ran in the Republican primary because I believe our district — and
Connecticut — deserved leadership rooted in respect, integrity, and
common sense. I was honored to earn 80 percent of the vote, sending a
clear message that hate and divisiveness have no place in our party or
our state. My experience in finance, as a business owner, and through
years of involvement in Pomfret civic life has prepared me to serve
effectively in the House of Representatives. Serving in various town
positions has shown me how state and local decisions affect families,
small businesses, taxpayers, and our communities. I will bring a
thoughtful, practical Republican voice to the State House — focused on
fiscal responsibility, accountable government, stronger communities, and
respectful leadership. I look forward to working hard for every
community in our district and earning the opportunity to represent you.”

**Talk about your background. What makes you stand out as the best
choice for State Representative?**

“I am a longtime Pomfret resident, small-business owner, and former
insurance executive who understands the challenges families and
employers face when costs rise and government becomes less accountable.
My background in finance and business has taught me to make responsible
decisions, balance priorities, and respect every taxpayer dollar. I have
also served our community as a former Pomfret selectman and Board of
Finance member. That experience gave me a firsthand understanding of
local budgets, public safety, schools, infrastructure, and the impact
that Hartford’s decisions have on small towns. What sets me apart is a
record of local service combined with real-world business experience. I
will bring a practical, respectful Republican voice to the State House —
one that listens, works across differences when it benefits our
district, and stands up for affordability, local control, and
accountable government.”

**Can you share a personal story or experience that highlights your
commitment to public service?**

“I have demonstrated a long-standing commitment to public service
through leadership roles that strengthen my local community, expand
educational opportunity, and support essential institutions. In Pomfret,
I have served on local boards and commissions and chaired the Pomfret
Fire building addition, helping support an important investment in
public safety and community infrastructure. That work reflects my belief
that effective public service often happens close to home — listening to
residents, planning responsibly, and helping ensure the community has
the facilities and services it needs. I have also supported healthcare
access and community well-being through Day Kimball Hospital, where I
served as chairman of the Development Committee. In that role, I helped
advance philanthropic efforts that support the hospital’s ability to
provide high-quality care to the people and families who depend on it. I
value the opportunity to contribute to an institution so central to the
region's health and resilience. Education has been another major focus
of my service. I participated in early scholarship committees and later
chaired the Rhode Island AGC Education Committee. One of the most
meaningful accomplishments of that work was helping develop the first
college-credit course **in** construction management. The goal was not
only to prepare students for careers in a vital industry, but also to
create a clearer pathway from education into skilled, meaningful
employment. Taken together, these experiences have reinforced my
commitment to public service as practical, collaborative work —
supporting public safety, healthcare, and education while helping create
opportunities that benefit the broader community.”

**What is the most important thing you would say to an undecided voter
heading to the polls on Nov. 3?**

“The most important thing I would say is this — your vote should be
about who will work hardest for your family, your town, and the future
of our district. I am running to bring practical experience, respectful
leadership, and common-sense solutions to Hartford. I understand that
families are concerned about taxes, energy costs, schools, roads, and
whether state government is listening to rural communities like ours. I
will be an accessible representative who listens to every resident —
Republican, Democrat, and unaffiliated — and puts the needs of the 50th
District ahead of political gamesmanship. My commitment is simple —
protect taxpayers, defend local control, support safe and strong
communities, and make Connecticut a more affordable place to live, work,
and raise a family. On November 3, I respectfully ask for the
opportunity to earn your trust and your vote.”
