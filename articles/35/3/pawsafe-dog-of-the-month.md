## Did You Know?

Just like humans are right- or left-handed, dogs can be right- or
left-pawed!

## Dog of the Month

Meet Miss Piggy! She is a 34-pound mix that loves other dogs and has
been in the shelter for more than two months. She's spayed, about 2
years old, and medically up-to-date. She’s adorable and is so hoping for
a family of her own!
