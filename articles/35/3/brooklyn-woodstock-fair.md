title: Eastford families enjoy the sights, sounds, and traditions of fair season
---

BROOKLYN/WOODSTOCK — Summer wouldn’t be complete without an annual trek
to the Quiet Corner’s local fairs. The 176th Annual Brooklyn Fair took
place from Aug. 27-30 at the Brooklyn Fairgrounds in Brooklyn, while the
165th Woodstock Fair took place on Labor Day weekend, Sept. 3-7, at the
Woodstock Fairgrounds in neighboring Woodstock. Several families from
Eastford enjoyed the fair, and *The Communicator* reached out to ask
them to send us their best photos! *Courtesy photos*
