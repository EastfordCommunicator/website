---
title: Recreation Commission Update
---

With fall in the air, we're excited for our Halloween Trunk or Treat on
Saturday, Oct. 31, at 6:30 p.m. in the Tow Office Building parking lot.
Bring the kids and enjoy. Or you can decorate your trunk and come help
out. If you want to bring your trunk, [please register in advance here](https://www.eastfordct.gov/recreation-commission).

This event is a great way for your kiddos to enjoy a safe Halloween
experience with all their friends. We are also looking forward to next
year’s Experience Eastford in June. We will be gathering a planning team
later in the fall. We plan to move the 5K and Fun Run to a fall date.

Finally, we are excited about new ways we can bring recreation events
and fun to Eastford. To do so, we need to grow our team. Maybe you would
like to help? Our meetings have moved to the third Wednesday of each
month at 6:30 p.m. Come to a meeting and see what it’s like.
