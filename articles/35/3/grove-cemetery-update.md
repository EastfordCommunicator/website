---
title: From the Eastford Grove Cemetery Association
---

Around 1960, the Grove Cemetery Association began selling plots to
Eastford residents in the newly developed south section. At the time,
only six-space, full burial plots were available in that section. If you
or your family purchased one of these, you may have much more space than
you would ever use. With family members moving out of town and the trend
of cremation rather than full burial, we believe there will,
unfortunately, be many vacant spots in the cemetery that can never be
used.

To clarify, a single plot space measures 4 feet by 9 feet and allows one
full vault burial plus two cremation burials, or three cremation
burials. In other words, the originally purchased, six-space plot can
accommodate six full burials, plus 12 cremation burials, or 18 cremation
burials.

Since Grove Cemetery has no expansion room beyond its present borders,
the Board of Trustees is urging plot owners to donate or sell (at the
original purchase price) unused plot space back to the Grove Cemetery
Association. Single spaces currently sell for more money ($1,200) than
they did 60 years ago, so the tax-deductible impact of donating plot
space could be sizeable. Since unused plot space cannot be sold by the
owner or inherited owners, or given away except to the GCA, please
consider donating unused space back to the Eastford Grove Cemetery
Association. Thank you!
