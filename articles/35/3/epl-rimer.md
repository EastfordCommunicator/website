---
title: Local author visits library to discuss ‘Back from Suicide’
---

EASTFORD — On Wednesday, Sept. 16, the Eastford Public Library hosted
Lisette Rimer, of Pomfret, the author of “Back from Suicide: Before and
After the Essential Patrick.” Joining her was Patrick’s twin sister,
Libby Antoinette Wood. Patrons and guests entered to the strains of
Patrick’s classical piano music playing quietly in the background. The
book chronicles the life and tragic death of Patrick Wood, a
valedictorian, AP Scholar, and National Merit Scholarship winner with
perfect SAT scores who graduated from Stanford. Despite being talented
and successful, Wood struggled with depression and tragically died by
suicide in 2006. Rimer wrote the book to educate readers about the
biology behind depression and the lessons she has learned since.
