---
title: Emergency Management Update
author: Deb Richards
---

On Tuesday, Sept. 1, Eastford Emergency Management participated in the
2026 governor-mandated Emergency Planning and Preparedness Initiative
(EPPI). The exercise focused on a severe weather event that progressed
from flooding to high winds, road closures, and downed trees.
Participants included James Roy, DEMD; Ben Schmidt, Public Works
Supervisor; Bernie Dub, Communications Officer; and Deborah Richards,
Emergency Management Director and First Selectman.

During the exercise, participants discussed their individual roles
during an emergency, the resources available to the town, and the
recordkeeping requirements necessary to apply for FEMA recovery funds.
After the drill, we discussed what we could do differently in future
exercises and reviewed new forms to use through WebEOC.

Thank you to everyone who participated and contributed their time and
expertise. If you are interested in participating in emergency
preparedness activities or serving on the Emergency Management
Committee, please contact <deborah.richards@eastfordct.gov>.
