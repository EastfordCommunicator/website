I continue to focus on affordability issues in a common-sense way. My
free e-newsletters and social media posts have shared a lot of
information about the work I have done over the past four years (more
than 35 specific items). There is more to do, which is why I remain
working on this important issue.

I have been rolling out my Affordability Plan full of proposals.
Although not all of them may pass, my proposal includes a wide array of
items we can work on to make Connecticut more affordable for you and
your family. By extension, by continuing the work done to increase
public and special education funding, town aid road money, and casino
fund money, this would mean more money to our towns, including Eastford,
that can offset the pressure on property taxes.

Some people have talked about increasing taxes, but the state can better
support our towns without raising taxes or cutting other important
services if it refocuses and repurposes the tax money it already has
toward core government responsibilities, like public education, instead
of spending it on things it does not need.
