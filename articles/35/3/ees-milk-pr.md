---
title: EES announces free milk eligibility guidelines
---

EASTFORD — The Eastford School District has announced its policy for
determining which children may receive free milk served under the
Special Milk Program (SMP). Local school officials have adopted the U.S.
Department of Agriculture’s (USDA) Income Eligibility Guidelines (IEGs)
for family size and income criteria.

Connecticut will use the following income guidelines from July 1, 2026,
to June 30, 2027, to determine participants’ eligibility for free milk
in the Child Nutrition Programs (CNP).

Students residing in households with income at or below the levels shown
in the chart below are eligible for free milk through the SMP. All
households received a family application via mail. To apply for free
milk through the SMP, households should complete the application and
return it to the school. Only one application is required per household
and an application for free milk cannot be approved unless it contains
complete eligibility information as indicated on the application and
instructions.

The information provided on the application is confidential and will be
used only to determine eligibility and to administer and enforce the
free milk program through the SMP. Additionally, all school-aged
students in income-eligible households can receive school milk benefits
regardless of a student’s immigration status and the district/school
does not release information for immigration-related purposes in the
usual course of operating the CNPs. Note that the district may share
your eligibility information with education, health, social service, and
nutrition programs to help them evaluate, fund, or determine benefits
for their programs, auditors for program reviews, and law enforcement
officials to help them look into violations of program rules. School or
other program officials may also verify this information at any time
during the school year. Applications may be submitted at any time during
the year.

No application is required if the district directly certifies a student
based on a household member receiving assistance from the SNAP or the
TFA program. All students in these households are eligible for free
school milk through the SMP. The school district will notify households
receiving assistance under the SNAP/TFA programs of their eligibility
and all enrolled students residing in the household will be offered free
school meals or free milk through the SMP unless the household notifies
the determining official that it chooses to decline benefits. If any
students were not listed on the eligibility notice, the household should
contact the district or school to have free milk benefits extended to
those students. Households receiving SNAP or TFA benefits for their
children should only submit an application if they are not notified of
their eligibility by September 30, 2026.

Some students may be directly certified for free milk through the SMP
based on HUSKY A (Medicaid). No application is required if the district
directly certifies a student based on participation in HUSKY A
(Medicaid). Students in these households are likely eligible for free
milk benefits and will be notified of their eligibility and their
children will be offered free milk through the SMP, unless the household
notifies the determining official that it chooses to decline benefits.
If any students were not listed on the eligibility notice, the household
should contact the district or school to have free milk benefits
extended to those students.

Additionally, some students may be eligible for free milk through the
SMP if the students were determined to be eligible for Summer Electronic
Benefits (S-EBT). No application is required if the district determines
that a student is eligible for free milk due to participation in S-EBT.
Students in these households are likely eligible for free milk benefits
and will be notified of their eligibility, and their children will be
offered free milk accordingly, unless the household notifies the
determining official that it chooses to decline benefits. If any
students were not listed on the eligibility notice, the household should
contact the district or school to have free milk benefits extended to
those students.

If a student is not directly certified, the household should complete
and submit a free application form. Households must provide proof of the
SNAP or TFA client ID number. An adult household member's signature is
also required.

Students in WIC may be eligible for free milk. Please complete and
submit a free milk application or contact the determining official for
more information.

When known to the district/school, households will be notified of any
student’s eligibility for free milk if the individual student is “Other
Source Categorically Eligible” because the student is categorized as
either: homeless; runaway as defined by law and determined by the
district’s or school’s homeless liaison; or enrolled in an eligible Head
Start or pre-kindergarten class as defined by law. Households with
students who are categorically eligible under Other Source Categorically
Eligible Programs should complete and submit a free milk application and
check off the relevant box. Direct questions to the determining
official.

For any student not listed on the eligibility notice, the households
should contact the school or determining official about any student also
eligible under one of these programs or should submit an income
application for the other students.

Households notified of their children’s eligibility must contact the
determining official or school if it chooses to decline the free school
milk benefits. If the district/school does not notify
households/students of their free milk benefits and they receive
benefits under qualifying public assistance programs or Other Source
Categorically Eligible Programs, the parent/guardian should contact the
determining official at their school.

Foster children that are under the legal responsibility of a foster care
agency or court are categorically eligible for free school milk. A
foster parent does not have to complete a free milk application if they
can submit a copy of the legal document or legal court order showing
that the student is a foster child. Additionally, a foster child may be
included as a member of the foster family if the foster family chooses
to also apply for benefits. If the foster family is not eligible for
free milk benefits, the foster child can still receive free milk. A
foster child’s eligibility for free school milk does not automatically
extend to all students in the household. Free milk application forms for
all other households require a statement of total household income,
household size, and names of all household members. Include the last
four digits of the Social Security number of an adult household member,
or a statement that the household member does not have one. The adult
household member must also sign the application certifying that the
information provided is correct.

Under the policy for determining eligibility for free and reduced-price
school meals, Principal Carole McCombe will review applications and
determine students’ eligibility for free milk. If a parent/guardian is
dissatisfied with the determining official's ruling, they may wish to
discuss the decision with the determining official informally. If they
wish to make a formal appeal, they may submit a request, either orally
or in writing, to Principal Carole McCombe at (860) 974-1130 or
<cmccombe@eastfordct.org> for a hearing to appeal the decision.

If a household member becomes unemployed or if household size changes at
any time, the household should contact the school to file a new free
milk application. These changes may make the household's students
eligible for free milk if household income falls at or below the levels
shown in the Income Eligibility Guidelines.

For questions about the free and reduced-price school meals application
process, contact the determining official at (860) 974-1130.
