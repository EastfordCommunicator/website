---
title: In the Garden
author: Susan Burns
---

I was speaking with a friend recently. She mentioned how her entire yard
is filled with plants. As a result, she is constantly working outside.
We talked about changing that in order to create areas for sitting.
Doing this doesn’t have to cost a lot of money. You can “pave” an area
with pine needles, traprock, bricks, Trex, concrete pavers, bluestone,
wood chips, or even sand. Then add a few chairs and a table for your
beverages, and you instantly have a place to relax instead of weeding,
watering, or deadheading.

In these times of drought or deluge, tending to a garden is becoming
more and more of a chore. This year’s summer was the hottest on record.
This made gardening extremely challenging. We need a place to cool off,
rest, and look at the garden instead of working in it.

Oh, and one more thing — don’t forget to finish your maintenance of
grass before Oct. 15. After that, planting seed, liming, or feeding will
be too late.
