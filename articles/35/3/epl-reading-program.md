---
title: Colonial Days Summer Reading Program participants honored
---

EASTFORD — Eastford Public Library recently honored adults who completed
the Colonial Days Summer Reading Program. Each person worked to complete
various reading and historical prompts on their America 250 BINGO cards.
The big winner was Kathy Healey, who completed the entire BINGO card.
Next was Ronald Jordan, who came in a very close second. Heather Riel
and Rebekah Budd (along with her husband John Shatos) came in third and
fourth place, respectively. Each winner received Early American-style
gifts in recognition of their commitment, love of reading and history,
and 1776 spirit. A very special thanks to the Woodstock Country Shoppe
for their generous donation and colonial inspiration.
