---
title: Voter Registration Session Announced
---

EASTFORD — A voter registration session will be held Friday, Oct. 16,
from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., in the lower level of the Town Office Building.
Early voting for the 2026 election will start on Monday, Oct. 19, and
continue until Sunday, Nov. 1, in the lower level of the Town Office
Building. Hours on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays
will be 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 8 a.m. to 8
p.m. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3, in the lower level of the Town
Office Building.
