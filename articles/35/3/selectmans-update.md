The Ashford Road bridge over Bigelow Brook project is moving along
quickly. With changes to reimbursement procedures from the Connecticut
Department of Transportation (CT DOT), along with changes to the
contracts originally signed by the towns, Eastford and other
participating towns now must assume many responsibilities previously
handled by CT DOT. These responsibilities include financing the project
in stages and then seeking reimbursement from the state.

To help manage this process and ensure Eastford maintains adequate cash
flow, the town has advertised for a financial advisor to review the
funds and cash flow currently available to the town. We are also
exploring a line of credit that we could use, if necessary, during peak
periods of the reimbursement process.

Many surrounding towns have already been through, or are currently going
through, this same process. We are learning from one another and working
closely with the financial administrators at NECCOG to navigate these
new requirements as effectively as possible.

Construction of the Ashford Road bridge is currently scheduled to begin
April 1, 2027. The town will post detour route information on its
website as it becomes available. One year after construction begins on
the Ashford Road bridge, construction is expected to begin on the Old
Colony bridge over Bungee Brook.

I want to take a moment to recognize and thank the phenomenal people who
work for the Town of Eastford. Every day, our employees give 110 percent
to ensure that our town runs smoothly, efficiently, and effectively.
Much of their work happens behind the scenes — work the public may never
see or fully realize. For that reason, I want to give all of our town
employees a well-deserved standing ovation and a heartfelt thank-you for
everything they do for Eastford and its residents. Your dedication,
commitment, and hard work do not go unnoticed. We truly appreciate
everything you do for our town!
