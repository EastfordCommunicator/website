---
title: Whitehouse named new town treasurer after Burgess resignation
author: Adam Minor
---

EASTFORD --- At a special meeting held Tuesday, Aug. 25, the Board of
Selectmen officially accepted the appointment of Amy Whitehouse as the
new town treasurer, replacing outgoing treasurer Martha Burgess, who
officially resigned on Aug. 3, along with Deputy Treasurer Kim Cyr.

The Republican Town Committee (chaired by Christopher Bowen) recommended
Whitehouse's appointment, which will allow her to finish Burgess'
current term. Burgess has served as Treasurer since her election in 2015
and was most recently elected uncontested in November 2025. According to
the Board of Selectmen's minutes from the Aug. 25 meeting, Whitehouse
will serve from Aug. 22 to Nov. 16, 2027. The 2027 election is slated
for Tuesday, Nov. 2, 2027.

In a statement to the *Communicator*, Whitehouse said she is "excited
and looking forward to this new opportunity to serve the Town of
Eastford as town treasurer." According to Whitehouse, she has worked for
the State of Connecticut for nearly 22 years in various financial
positions. She is currently an Associate Fiscal Administrative Officer
(AFAO) for the Department of Transportation in the Bureau of Finance and
Administration. Before that, she worked in banking for four years at the
Savings Bank of Manchester (now Key Bank).

Additionally, at the Board of Selectmen meeting on Monday, Sept. 14,
selectmen accepted Whitehouse's recommendation to appoint Amber Pond as
the new Deputy Treasurer. According to state statute, the Treasurer has
the authority to appoint their own deputy.
