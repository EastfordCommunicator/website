### Saturday, Oct. 10

Buell’s Orchard will host its annual Harvest Festival on Saturday, Oct.
10, and Monday, Oct. 12, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Festival activities
include wagon rides to apple and pumpkin picking, live music by Shady
Creek Band, a chicken barbecue, apple cider donuts, face painting, and
much more! A fun family time for all! This event will be held rain or
shine, unless there are high winds or downpours. No pets allowed. No
entry fee or parking fee! Buck's Soft Serve trailer will also be here
all weekend. Some, but not all, of the festival activities will be
available on Sunday, Oct. 11, between 1-5 p.m.

### Sunday, Oct. 11

On Sunday, Oct. 11 and 25, from 2-4 p.m., as part of Last Green Valley’s
“Walktober” event, come check out the Florence Warren Latham Museum of
Eastford History's new exhibit — an interactive 1/34 scale model of
Eastford's 1823 Phoenix Stone Cotton Mill! Created by Michael Yurkovic
and admired by folks at the Slater Museum in Pawtucket and the Mill
Museum in Windham, it reflects the migration of early American
industrial technology know-how and investment across Rhode Island and
into Eastern Connecticut. Private tours Saturdays by appointment.
Sponsored by the Eastford Historical Society. [Visit
this webpage for more information](https://www.eastfordct.gov/community/page/eastford-historical-society).

### Saturday, Oct. 17

On Saturday, Oct. 17, Windham-Tolland 4-H Camp will hold its 5th Annual
Fall Extravaganza from 2-4 p.m. During teen weekend, teens help decorate
the cabins on the trick-or-treat trail and run games, hand out candy,
and happily give tours to visitors at this fun event. Admission is $5
per person, ages 2 and up. Your admission fee includes s’mores and hot
chocolate at the fire pit by the pond. We’ll also have other games to
keep visitors occupied, and some sweet treats will be for sale. The
donut eating contest is always a hit. Everyone is welcome on the
trick-or-treat trail and to get an early start on their Halloween candy
collection. No worries about traffic or walking around in the dark. It
is the perfect time to break in this year’s costume and take some great
photos. Proceeds benefit the Health Center Renovation. This is a great
family event and a good chance to check out our camp facilities, enjoy
the fall foliage, and meet some of our staff. Windham-Tolland 4-H Camp
is located at 326 Taft Pond Rd., Pomfret Center, and may be contacted at
(860) 974-1122 or online at [www.4hcampct.org](http://www.4hcampct.org).
