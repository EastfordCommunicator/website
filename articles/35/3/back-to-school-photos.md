title: Off they go! Eastford students head back to school
---

EASTFORD — The summer season is now nothing but a memory. Eastford
Elementary School welcomed students back to class on Thursday, Aug. 27,
which gave parents plenty of photo opportunities. With this in mind,
*The Communicator* asked its readers to submit their best “back to
school” photos of their kids, and as usual — they did not disappoint!
All of us at *The* *Communicator* wish all EES students a great school
year! *Courtesy photos*
