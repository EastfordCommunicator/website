---
title: Auction set to support eighth-grade Washington, D.C. trip
author: Adam Minor
---

EASTFORD — Every spring, eighth graders from Eastford Elementary School
make a pilgrimage to Washington, D.C. for a multi-day field trip that
brings our nation’s history alive and creates memories that last a
lifetime.

Planning for the field trip happens year-round, and over the years, the
trip has grown increasingly expensive to pull off — especially since
funding is traditionally raised so no students have to pay out of
pocket.

Thankfully, this is where the annual Goods and Services Auction comes
in.

This year’s event, which is in its 19th year, is scheduled for Saturday,
Nov. 14, with doors opening at 5 p.m. Along with a live auction, silent
auction, and raffles, the event will include a bake sale, food by Pizza
101, and much more. Last year’s event, which benefited both the seventh-
and eighth-grade trip last May (classes were combined for the trip due
to small class size), raised approximately $15,000, and according to
Lauren Barlow, who co-chairs the Auction Committee with her husband,
Jeremy, increasing costs are making the success of the auction even more
important this year.

“The goal has always been to fully fund the eighth-grade class trip to
Washington, D.C.,” she said, adding that last year’s trip cost nearly
$30,000 just for transportation and the group’s hotel stay. The auction
helped relieve some of that pressure, but it could no longer be relied
on as the sole source of fundraising revenue. Thankfully, local parents
stepped up to fill the gap, holding a “Someone Special Dance,” chocolate
sales, and Pampered Chef fundraisers to fill the remaining funds.

“The auction is a single event,” Barlow said. “It cannot sustain the
trip long term \[anymore\]. That doesn’t mean we are ready to throw in
the towel.”

One of the major keys to the auction’s success is getting local
businesses to donate gift certificates, services, and other items.

“Participation is key,” she said. “The big gap means we need people to
go out and solicit businesses. We need people to take specific towns.
The other thing is, we want to get more people that have never attended
the auction before. We put out signs and publicity, but if anyone has
friends and family from surrounding towns, bring them.”

Gift cards from businesses all around the Quiet Corner and beyond are a
staple of the auction, but Barlow said she is particularly interested in
items from local artists.

“I want to see more unique artisan pieces,” she said, adding that the
Eastford Public Library has been particularly helpful in finding
talented artists.

While the items themselves are a reason to participate, Barlow added
that businesses have an added incentive to join in, as the event gives
them a chance to get their names out to local residents.

“This is a great chance to showcase local business,” she said.

The main event of the evening will be the annual live auction. Barlow
said the Auction Committee has already collected various items to be
auctioned off, including a hockey puck signed by Boston Bruins
defenseman Jordan Harris, tickets to Quassy Amusement & Waterpark, right
field tickets to the Hartford Yard Goats, a family pass to Beardsley
Zoo, a four-person voucher to the Essex Steam Train, New York Giants
memorabilia, and more school-related experiences, such as a ride to
school on a fire truck, a VIP Graduation experience, Principal for a
Day, and an opportunity to pie a Board of Education member in the face.
Pizza will be provided by Pizza 101, and a bake sale will also be
available.

The next Washington, D.C. trip will be held in spring 2028, as this
year’s eighth graders joined last year’s trip as seventh graders. But
while there is additional time to raise money for the 2028 trip, Barlow
said this does not mean the Auction Committee is planning to take their
foot off the gas pedal.

“Come to the auction,” she said. “And bring two people that have never
been before. Otherwise, we are doing this in a vacuum. We need people
who haven’t been here before to generate more interest. You will have a
fun time. It is entertaining, and it is a good time to shop before
Christmas and the holidays. You might even end up getting a really good
deal on something pretty cool and unique.”

For more information or to inquire about donating an item for the
auction, contact the committee at <auction@eastfordct.org>.
