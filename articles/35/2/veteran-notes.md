Could you use a little extra financial help? The Soldiers, Sailors and
Marines Fund has expanded its financial assistance programs for any
Connecticut veteran or surviving spouse who served during a wartime
period.

You may qualify for assistance with:
- Monthly rent
- Electric and utility bills
- A free tank of heating oil
- And other financial needs

For example, veterans who served in the U.S. Army in Germany between
1961 and 1975 may be eligible. You do not need to have served in a
combat zone to qualify.

To be eligible, a couple's household income should generally not exceed
$80,000 per year.

There is no cost to apply, and all inquiries are handled with the utmost
confidentiality.

For more information or to see if you qualify, contact Garry Brown,
Municipal Veteran Representative, at (860) 928-2309 or e-mail
<garry.brown71@aim.com>.
