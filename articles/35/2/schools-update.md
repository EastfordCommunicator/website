---
title: From the Superintendent
author: Donna Leake
---

I always enjoy the excitement and special energy that permeates the
first days of school. As we begin the 2026-27 school year, I want to
take a moment to thank our entire school community for helping to make
the opening of this school year a success. Our students returned ready
to learn, connect, and grow. It is uplifting to see students
reconnecting with their friends and meeting their new teachers. Our
teachers and staff have worked diligently to prepare welcoming
classrooms and supportive learning environments. They deserve a special
thank you for their dedication and commitment to our students.

In addition to welcoming back our returning staff members, we are
pleased to welcome Mrs. Dana Mutch as our Director of Special Programs
and Social Studies teacher for grades 5 and 6, and Mr. Stephen Nappi as
the Special Education teacher who will be leading a new program for us
--- the Therapeutic Learning Center (TLC), which is designed to meet the
varied learning needs of students at EES. Although not new to the staff,
Catherine DePercio, our world language teacher, will also serve as the
social studies teacher for grades 7 and 8.

In addition to hiring new staff, our administration has been actively
monitoring several facilities projects this summer. With a new coat of
paint, our gym has become a brighter and more inviting learning and
community space. The overall appearance of EES has been enhanced with
the installation of a new roof on the middle school section of the
building, and our cupola has been restored.

At its meeting on Aug. 20, the Board of Education (BOE) recognized the
recipients of the Community Foundation Scholarships --- Robert
Ellsworth, Emmanuel Kaberia, Eric Matheson, Johanna Blanchette, Max
Larkin, and Kerry Blais. The BOE also received a special report
concerning high school options from Lauren Barlow and Terry Cote,
members of the Ad Hoc High School Committee. Additional review and
discussion of this topic will take place during the Community
Conversation scheduled for Sept. 24, from 6-8 p.m.

In closing, I need to thank our families and members of the Eastford
community for your continuing partnership. In a small community like
ours, we know that it takes all of us working together to provide a
successful learning environment for all students. I am grateful for the
support, kindness, and pride you continue to show for our schools. As
John Dewey reminded us, "Education is not preparation for life;
education is life itself." This is a wonderful thought as we begin this
new year. Every day brings our students opportunities to learn,
discover, build relationships, and become the people they are meant to
be. I am looking forward to a wonderful year ahead full of learning,
growth, and many moments that make us proud to be part of this
community.

Thank you for allowing me to be a part of this amazing learning
community. As always, you are invited to contact me at
<dleake@eastfordct.org> if you have questions or concerns and/or would
like to schedule an appointment. Of course, please feel welcome to stop
by to say hello before our regularly scheduled Board of Education
meetings. Our next meeting is Sept. 10 at 6 p.m. in the EES library.
