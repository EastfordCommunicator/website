---
title: Auction Committee Seeks Assistance from Community
---

EASTFORD --- The Eastford School Annual Goods & Services Auction is
coming up on Saturday, Nov. 14, and we're starting to collect donations
for our silent and live Auctions.

For those who may not know, the auction helps fund the eighth-grade
class trip to Washington, D.C. (or, when class sizes are smaller, the
seventh- and eighth-grade trip together). Our goal is to raise enough
money to make sure every student has the opportunity to go, regardless
of their family's ability to pay. We really don't want any student to
miss out because of the cost.

We reach out to businesses all over Connecticut and New England, but
we'd especially love to support and highlight the talented people and
small businesses right here in Eastford and our surrounding communities.

Do you make pottery, jewelry, art, or woodwork? Own a landscaping
business, food truck, salon, or pet service? Maybe you're a
photographer, baker, personal trainer, yoga instructor, or have another
service or talent that could make a great auction item.

We'd love to hear from you! Donations can be an item, a gift
certificate, a service, an experience, or anything else you think people
would enjoy bidding on.

If you're interested in donating or just want to learn more, please
e-mail <auction@eastfordct.org>.

Thank you to everyone who has supported this auction over the years. It
really does take a community to make this happen, and we're so grateful
for ours!
