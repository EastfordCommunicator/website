---
title: "From the Eastford Independent Fire Company #1, Inc."
---

Where did the summer go? Hot and humid weather is still predicted --- so
hydrate, hydrate, hydrate!

The EIFC responded to 24 calls during July.

Annual hose and ladder testing was scheduled on Aug. 1. The purpose of
the testing was to determine how much pressure the hoses can take and
the strength of the ladders.

Santa returned to the Peppertree Campground on Aug. 8.

On a solemn note, members, along with Engine Tanker 171, Rescue 171, and
UTV, participated in the annual "Silas Run" in memory of member EMS
Lieutenant Silas Bunnell, who passed away in April 2018. Silas joined
the EIFC as a Junior Member in 2013. He took pride in his work, offering
his computer expertise and being only a phone call away to support his
peers and fellow officers. Silas became proficient as a pump operator on
Engine Tanker 171 and earned the rank of CAR 971 in June of 2017. There
were approximately 300 people in attendance at the J.J. Webster Scout
Reservation in Ashford for a "Celebration of Life" run honoring Silas on
July 11. Run fees and donations are contributed to a fund in his name to
help subsidize scholarships for potential Scout campers. There is always
a true sense of fellowship and support from friends, family, and local
fire departments in memory of Silas during the "Silas Run." RIP -- CAR
971.

*-Submitted by Jeannine Spink*
