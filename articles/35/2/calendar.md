## September

### Wednesday, Sept. 2

Wednesday night ministries are slated to begin on Wednesday, Sept. 2, at
Eastford Baptist Church! There is something for all ages! For Grades
1-6, join us at 6:30 p.m. for our Olympians children's ministry. For
students in Grades 7-12, join us in the Family Activity Center at 6:30
p.m. for Teen Club! For adults, join us at 6:30 p.m. for Women's
Ministry, where we will be beginning a study in the Book of James. In
the downstairs Family Activity Center classroom, we will begin a new
series titled "Cults and Counterfeit Christianity." We will discuss the
definition of a "cult" and then look at several common examples. For
more information, call the church at (860) 974-1414, or visit
www.eastfordbaptist.com.

Save the Date! Thursday, Sept. 24, 6-8 p.m. in the Eastford Elementary
School Gymnasium --- The Eastford Board of Education invites all
Eastford families and community members to attend a Community
Conversation regarding the district\'s ongoing review of Eastford\'s
designated high school. For the past several months, the board has been
comparing and evaluating Woodstock Academy and E.O. Smith High School to
better understand the opportunities each school offers Eastford
students. This meeting is an opportunity to learn more, ask questions,
and share your thoughts as part of the community discussion. Your voice
is important. Whether you are a current parent, future parent, student,
resident, or community member, we encourage you to attend and
participate in this conversation. This informational meeting is intended
to provide information and gather community input. No decision will be
made at this event.
