When we hosted a Garden Conservancy Open Day here this summer, most
guests asked about one plant in particular.

This plant is "Clethra alnifolia," whose common names are Summersweet
and Sweet Pepperbush. This is a native plant, and really good for
wildlife. When it blooms, it smells heavenly. Clethra is very easy to
grow. I have never watered it. We have a hedge of the shrub. It is
tremendously pest-free. It is excellent for summer flowers and
fragrance, a shrub border, and good in shade and wet areas, although
ours are grown in drought conditions. The foliage is very handsome, and
the overall winter habit is clean. This is one of the easiest plants to
grow at commercial and garden centers, so many cultivars are available.
You could choose pink or white flowers --- whichever height suits your
landscape and your soil conditions. Our hedge uses the cultivar
"Hummingbird," which stays low and spreads by rhizomes. Fall color is
golden yellow. The flowers are 6 inches long and open earlier than the
parent (species). This plant has cold hardiness of -30°F.

So, if you are looking for something special for your yard that attracts
a lot of attention, look no further than Clethra. A picture of this
plant is attached to this article.
