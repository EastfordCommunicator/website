---
title: EPL Celebrates Summer Reading Finale with Colonial-Themed Event
---

EASTFORD --- It was a perfect summer Saturday on Aug. 15, as the
Eastford Public Library celebrated its Colonial Days Summer Reading
Finale with old-fashioned fun and games in the park. Children crafted
the 19th-century Game of Graces using wooden embroidery hoops, ribbons,
and dowels. They played with colonial toys and cat's cradle, visited
the park's StoryWalk, experimented with three-legged racing, blew
bubbles, enjoyed refreshing ice cream and frozen pops, and held friendly
tug-of-war competitions.
