---
title: Windham-Tolland 4-H Camp News
---

Despite the heat, there was lots of fun and laughter, friendships made,
and new skills learned at camp this summer. We ran at close to full
capacity all summer, with 826 children attending for more than 1,475
camper weeks.

Come check out our Fall Tag Sale on Saturday, Sept. 26, at the Horse
Camp entrance from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. We will have a wide range of donated
items --- furniture, vintage and antique, holiday, golf clubs, household
and decorative goods, linens, toys, handmade cradle, and more.

As our summer program ends, we shift our focus to our Ragged Hill Woods
Environmental Education Programs. Our fall homeschool days for children
in Grades 2-5 will run from 12:30 to 3:30 p.m. on Mondays as follows:
- Sept. 14 -- Nature Detective Training
- Sept. 21 -- Mapmakers and Trailblazers
- Sept. 28 -- Tree Time Travelers
- Oct. 5 -- Forest Feasts: Seeds, Nuts, and Survival
- Oct. 19 -- Forest Floor Recycling Crew
- Oct. 26 -- Rocks, Roots and Hidden Trails

Cost is $45 per child per class or $250 for all six weeks. Topics are
being selected for our six-week Wednesday afternoon programs, held from
4:30 to 5:30 p.m., for children in kindergarten to Grade 6, from Sept. 9
to Oct. 21. All programs are held rain or shine. You can e-mail
<raggedhillwoods@gmail.com> for topics, fees, and specific dates for this
series.

As pictured with this article, the Ragged Hill Woods Program was
recently awarded a $5,000 grant from the Chelsea Groton Foundation. We
are extremely grateful for this award, which will be used to keep
tuition costs as low as possible, allowing more participants to access
the program. This is the second year we have received support from this
foundation, and as you can see, it has made many campers smile.

Windham-Tolland 4-H Camp is located at 326 Taft Pond Rd, Pomfret Center,
and can be contacted at (860) 974-1122 or visited online at
www.4hcampct.org.
