---
title: The Pastor's Pen
author: Timothy Howard Jr.
---

A simple invitation may not seem like much. It is a little like planting
a seed --- the act takes only a moment, but we never know what may grow
from it. An invitation to church may introduce a child to the truth of
God's Word, help a teenager find lasting friendships and direction, or
encourage an adult who has quietly been searching for hope.

That is why, as pastor of Eastford Baptist Church, I would like to
extend a simple invitation to you and your family --- come and grow with
us.

Every Sunday begins with Bible studies for all ages at 9:15 a.m. These
classes provide an opportunity to study the Bible carefully, ask
meaningful questions, and discover how God's Word applies to everyday
life. At 10:30 a.m., we gather for worship, prayer, fellowship, and
in-depth, verse-by-verse preaching from Scripture.

Also, beginning Sept. 2, we meet every Wednesday at 6:30 p.m. Our
midweek gathering includes Bible study for adults, our Olympians
children's ministry (Grades 1-6), and Teen Club (Grades 7-12). Children
and teenagers enjoy age-appropriate Bible lessons, games, activities,
and time with friends in a safe and welcoming environment. Our goal is
to help young people build a strong biblical foundation while forming
healthy friendships and having fun together.

In a busy and often confusing world, individuals and families need the
unchanging truth and lasting hope found in Jesus Christ. Whether you
have attended church your entire life, have been away for some time, or
have never entered a church before, you will be warmly welcomed at
Eastford Baptist Church.

Sometimes something as simple as accepting an invitation can become the
beginning of something life-changing. We invite you to come, meet us,
and discover a place where your whole family can learn, belong, and
grow.
