---
title: "EBC Goes 'Down on the Farm' for Latest VBS Event"
---

EASTFORD --- Eastford Baptist Church brought the farm to its latest
Vacation Bible School event Aug. 3-7, as it held its annual outreach
week for more than 50 kids in grades pre-K to 6. The theme of the week
was "Down on the Farm: God Gives the Growth," which featured a weeklong
curriculum based on the parables of Jesus. Kids enjoyed snacks, crafts,
games, music, live animals, and giveaways, along with a "Water Night"
theme night on Thursday, Aug. 6. Also featured throughout the week was
the church's annual Penny War and Food Drive to benefit the Eastford
Food Pantry. This year, the children raised $700 and collected more
than 300 items for the food pantry. Next year's event has already been
announced for July 26-30, 2027, and will be titled: "Hero: God to the
Rescue."
