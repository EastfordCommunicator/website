---
title: Sports Camp Brings Spirit-Filled Summer Fun to EES
---

EASTFORD --- The Congregational Church of Eastford and Living Proof
Church in Ashford held their annual Sports Camp at Eastford Elementary
School July 27-31. Sports included flag football, basketball, cheer,
soccer, and skill development for 4- and 5-year-olds. Campers received
daily Bible lessons. Staff included college-aged coach/leaders from UW
Sports Ministries. The week hosted 137 kids and around 50 volunteers.
This was the eighth year of the camp.
