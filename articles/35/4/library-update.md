Our Annual November Food Drive to support the important work of the
Eastford Food Pantry is underway. Here’s a reminder of the kinds of
items that would be helpful:
- Protein-rich foods, such as canned tuna, canned salmon, canned
chicken, peanut butter, sun butter, canned or dried beans;
- Grains, such as rice, pasta, oats, cereals, and crackers;
- Canned goods, such as fruit in water or juice, vegetables, soups, and
stews;
- Basics, such as oils, spices, low-sodium broths, flour, and sugar;
- Pasta sauce, shelf-stable milk, kid-friendly snacks like granola bars;
- Personal care items, such as soap, deodorant, toothpaste,
toothbrushes, and feminine hygiene products;
- Baby supplies such as baby formula and baby food…
- Household supplies, such as cleaning supplies and paper products.

We appreciate your help supporting our neighbors in need. Every donation
makes a difference.

Reminder! The Friends of the Eastford Public Library will hold their
annual Holiday Fair on Saturday, Dec. 5, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. All
proceeds support the library!

Library hours are Tuesdays, 11 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.; Wednesdays, 10 a.m. to
5 p.m.; Thursdays, 11 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.; and Saturdays, 9 a.m. to 12
p.m. Stop in — we’d love to see you!
