---
title: Buell’s Orchard celebrates autumn with annual Harvest Festival
---

EASTFORD — Buell’s Orchard enjoyed picturesque, sunny weather over
Columbus Day weekend, perfect for their annual Harvest Festival on
Saturday, Oct. 10, and Monday, Oct. 12, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The
multi-day event, which draws thousands of people from across New
England, featured a chicken barbecue, face painting, live music by Nick
Anderson and Shady Creek, wagon rides, apple and pumpkin picking, and
ice cream from “Buck’s on the Run.” On Sunday, Oct. 11, the orchard was
open from 1-5 p.m., with a food truck by Poultry in Motion.
