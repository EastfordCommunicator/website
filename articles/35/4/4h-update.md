Our Ragged Hill Woods environmental science winter program schedule is
off and running. We offer homeschool programs on Mondays for children in
grades 6 to 8 from 8 to 11 a.m., and for grades 2 to 5 from 12:30 to
3:30 p.m., as follows:
- Nov. 2 – Spooky Forest Survival
- Nov. 9 – Mystery Animal Cases
- Nov. 16 – Hidden Homes and Secret Spaces
- Nov. 23 – Shelter Builder Challenge
- Nov. 30 – Cold Weather Survival Lab
- Dec. 7 – First Signs of Winter

Cost is $45 per child per class or $250 for all six classes.

On Wednesdays from 4:30 to 5:30 p.m. for Pre-K to grade 8 students, we
have the following programs scheduled:
- Oct. 28 – Predator vs. Prey
- Nov. 4 – Density
- Nov. 11 – Animal Eyes
- Nov. 18 – Peppermint Beetles
- Dec. 2 – Winter Ponds
- Dec. 9 – Create your own cookies.

Cost is $20 per child per class or $110 for all six weeks. All programs
are held rain or shine. You can e-mail raggedhillwoods@gmail.com or call
(860) 974-1122 to register for any of these programs.

Join us for our traditional Lunch with Santa fundraiser scheduled for
Sunday, Dec. 6, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m., to start your holiday season
early. Enjoy lunch upstairs in the lodge, or come for a photo with Santa
and skip the lines at the mall. Enjoy our made-to-order pasta bar,
salad, drinks, and desserts (gluten-free pasta available). Join us in
making holiday crafts, cookies, s’mores, and more, and shop our
selection of silent auction and raffle items. Tickets are available
ahead of time by calling (860) 974-3379, e-mail registrar@4hcampct.org,
or at the door. No reservations necessary. Lunch tickets are $15 for
those 10 and older, $10 for children age 9 and under. A 5x7 photo with
Santa is $8. Funds raised support the Jim Logee Campership Fund.

It’s never too early to think about booking a great summer camp
experience for your kids. Registration opens Jan. 1, 2027, on our
website, [www.4hcampct.org](https://4hcampct.org/). Gift certificates for any amount can be
applied to camp registration fees and make great gifts for family and
friends. These can be purchased by calling our registrar at (860)
974-3379 any time of the year.

Windham-Tolland 4-H Camp is located at 326 Taft Pond Rd., Pomfret
Center. Contact us at (860) 974-1122 or visit [www.4hcampct.org](https://4hcampct.org/).
