*Editor’s Note: This edition was sent to press on Monday, Oct. 26, which
was before the Eastford Bulldogs’ final regular season game against
Ashford on Tuesday, Oct. 27. Therefore, we could not include results
from that game or any potential playoff reporting. A complete season
recap is planned for the December edition of the Communicator.*

EASTFORD — For both the boys' and girls' Bulldogs soccer teams, the 2026
season has been a series of ups and downs, with 11 of 12 regular-season
games played as of press time Monday, Oct. 26.

The boys’ team, led by Head Coach Kullen Pacheco, started out on a high
note, starting the season 2-0 with wins over St. James (2-1) on Sept. 15
and Parish Hill (2-0) on Sept. 17. Kameron Pacheco jumped out early as
the team’s scoring leader, netting three goals in the first two games.

The Bulldogs faltered in the next four games, however, as they lost at
home to Canterbury, 4-1, on Tuesday, Sept. 22, and were then shut out,
8-0, on the road at Thompson on Thursday, Sept. 24. These losses were
followed by a loss to Pomfret, 4-2, at home on Sept. 29, and an overtime
loss at Ashford, 4-3, on Oct. 1 — leaving the Bulldogs holding on for
playoff positioning at 2-4 at the midway point of the season.

After defeating St. James at home, 3-2, on Oct. 8, to move to 3-4, the
Bulldogs would go on to lose three straight — a 1-0 loss at Parish Hill
on Oct. 13, a 5-2 loss at Canterbury on Oct. 14, and a 7-2 loss against
Thompson at home on Oct. 20 — to move to 3-7. On Oct. 21, the Bulldogs
traveled to Pomfret, a perennial QVJC power, and shocked the Eagles with
a 5-4 come-from-behind win (after being down 4-1 at halftime), moving
the Bulldogs to 4-7 (2-3 home, 2-4 away) as of press time. Over the
middle and latter portion of the season, Sylas Platt emerged as the
team’s scoring leader, with 9 goals as of Oct. 26. Besides Platt and
Pacheco, Charlie Ellsworth (3), Jack Kernan (2), and Logan Gillett (2)
have also contributed scores to the Bulldogs’ team total.

On the girls’ side, the Bulldogs, led by Head Coach Jen Barlow,
struggled at the start, losing their first five games of the season — a
6-0 home loss to St. James on Sept. 15; a close, 2-1 loss at Parish Hill
on Sept. 17; a 9-0 loss at Canterbury on Sept. 22; a 4-1 loss at Pomfret
on Sept. 29, and a 5-0 loss at home to Ashford on Oct. 1. However, the
girls would finally break through against Thompson on Oct. 7, shutting
out the Tigers 3-0 to notch their first win of the season. They would
follow up that win with another victory, a 1-0 win at St. James to move
to 2-5.

Back-to-back 3-0 losses versus Parish Hill (Oct. 13) and Canterbury
(Oct. 14) would drop the Bulldogs to 2-7, but in a rematch with
Thompson, the Bulldogs fought to a 2-2 draw, moving their record to
2-7-1. On Oct. 21, the girls dropped their home finale to Pomfret, 6-1.

Hayleigh Wooding has emerged as the team’s scoring leader with three
goals. Scarlett Haynes also scored two goals, while Olivia DePercio,
Chloe Minor, Aiyana Reed, and Emma-Lynn Salony each scored one.
