The EIFC responded to 32 “911” emergencies in September.

In addition to the monthly business meeting, truck check, and Line
Officers meeting, drills included EpiPen administration, hose and ladder
advancement, and extrication at Benway’s Auto in Pomfret.

An EpiPen delivers a fast intramuscular injection of epinephrine to open
airways, raises blood pressure, and reduces swelling. Anaphylaxis can be
caused by foods, medications, and insect stings. Administration of the
EpiPen is essential at the first signs of difficulty breathing, swelling
of the face/lips, rash, hives, throat tightness, and is the primary
life-saving treatment. Call 911.

Hose/Ladder Advancement is a firefighting skill where firefighters
stretch and move a hose line to a fire area. Ladders are often used to
assist in delivering water for attack, overhaul or ventilation.

Extrication drills are structured training exercises that practice the
skills, procedures, and teamwork needed to safely remove a trapped
person from a dangerous situation, most often caused by a crashed
vehicle. The goal of extrication drilling is to build proficiency,
coordination, and safety for the victim and responders.

The next holiday we observe is Veterans Day. Veterans Day, originally
known as Armistice Day, is a federal holiday in the United States
observed annually on Nov. 11 to honor U.S. military veterans. In 1954,
Armistice Day was changed to Veterans Day. A growing number of U.S.
states recognize Women Veterans Recognition Day (observed on June 12) to
honor women who have served in the U.S. military. Veterans Day differs
from Memorial Day in May in that it celebrates the service of all U.S.
veterans, while Memorial Day honors and mourns U.S. military personnel
who died while serving in the U.S. Armed Forces.

Cemeteries around the country display the U.S. flag and service markers
as a symbol of this recognition. Grove Cemetery proudly honors these
heroes. When you pass the cemetery, take a moment to reflect on what
Veterans Day means for securing our freedom. The red flags honor
deceased fire department members. All flags will be removed from the
cemetery on Nov. 12.

The holidays and cooking are right around the corner. Choose decorations
that are flame-resistant or flame-retardant. As you plan your
Thanksgiving menu, don’t forget fire safety. Thanksgiving is a peak day
for home-cooking fires. By following safety rules, you will have a
delicious and fire-safe holiday.

The EIFC has four requests:

1. Mark your house number by displaying the free, green sign with the
white reflective number, especially important for houses with multi-use
driveways.

2. Keep the area of the entrance and signs free of debris and snow
drifts.

3. When the time changes, replace all batteries in smoke and carbon
monoxide detectors.

4. To ensure prompt and efficient medical service and transfer, it is
essential to have a document available listing health issues,
medications that are taken, and for what purpose. This information will
be passed on to the ambulance crew and to the receiving hospital
emergency room. This simple document will assure our quick and efficient
response to your needs. Ready today — preparing for tomorrow!
