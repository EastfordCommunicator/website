---
title: Painting class gives community a brush with creativity at EBC
---

EASTFORD — On Friday, Sept. 25, Eastford Baptist Church hosted a
Community Painting Class. More than 25 community members attended the
class, led by EBC Pastor Timothy Howard Jr., who has been painting for
years. Attendees painted a scenic lake in acrylics using rich blues,
purples, and pinks, and also enjoyed refreshments throughout the
two-hour class. The church hosts Community Painting Classes regularly,
so visit [www.eastfordbaptist.com](http://www.eastfordbaptist.com) for
more information.
