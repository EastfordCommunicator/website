November is a time to celebrate accomplishments, give thanks, and
reflect on the work still ahead. It is a chance to recognize what is
going well, consider the challenges we have encountered, and adjust our
plans as needed.

- Eastford Elementary Highlights: At Eastford Elementary School, our
continued focus is on providing every student with educational
opportunities that recognize their individual strengths, learning
styles, and needs. Recognizing and celebrating the many talents of our
students is an important priority at Eastford Elementary School. We are
proud to share that six of our students have been selected to
participate in the Quinebaug Valley Music Festival, held at Plainfield
High School on Nov. 2 and 4. The festival brings together outstanding
middle school musicians from northeastern Connecticut. Music Director
Kelley Farrell-Denke selected these students for their musical skills,
positive attitudes, and dedication to our school music program.
Congratulations to seventh grader Owen Barlow and eighth graders Georgia
Barlow, Myckenzie Coldwell, Olivia DePercio, Chloe Minor, and Grace
Witter! We are proud of their accomplishments and grateful to Ms.
Farrell-Denke for supporting and encouraging our students’ musical
growth.

As we enter this season of thanks, I would be remiss not to recognize
the ongoing support of our families and our Parent-Teacher Organization
(PTO). One way the PTO supports our school is through its annual sign
contest, which gives students the chance to create the winning design
for the insert on our school sign near Westford Road. This year’s theme
is “Kindness in Action.” Students were invited to illustrate what
kindness looks like at Eastford Elementary School. The theme reflects a
belief that is central to our school community — everyone deserves to be
treated with respect and kindness. These values are woven into our
curriculum and daily interactions. We also teach them explicitly as we
help students navigate disagreements, resolve conflicts, and learn to
understand one another. By reinforcing these values each day, we
continue to build the positive school culture and climate that make
Eastford Elementary School such a special place to learn and grow. As
the quote often attributed to Aristotle reminds us, “Educating the mind
without educating the heart is no education at all.” We look forward to
unveiling the new school sign and announcing the winning artist once the
new sign insert returns from the printer.

Each year, the Eastford Elementary Auction Committee generously gives
its time and energy to organize and coordinate the annual Eastford
Auction. The committee’s dedication and the enthusiastic support of our
staff, families, and community members make this event possible. The
proceeds provide substantial support for our upper-level students’ trip
to Washington, D.C., helping make this valuable educational experience
possible. This year's auction will take place on the evening of
Saturday, Nov. 14. We are grateful to everyone who contributes their
time, resources, and generosity to make this event a success.

- Board of Education Update: Our Board of Education continues its
important work on behalf of our students, staff, and community. Although
much of this work happens behind the scenes, the board’s decisions play
an essential role in supporting our schools, guiding district
priorities, and ensuring that we have the resources and plans in place
to serve our students well.

At its Oct. 8 meeting, the board approved the following:

- The 2027 Board of Education meeting dates;

- Submission of the School Safety and Security Plan;

- Ratification of the 2027–2030 ETA contract;

- Purchase of supplemental reading materials for Grade 4;

- Proposed transfers in the FY2027 budget.

The board also received updates and presentations on several important
topics:

- Regional Collaboration and Advocacy: Board member Michael Bilica
shared a report from the Area Board Chair meetings held in Putnam. This
regional group brings together Board of Education members from towns
throughout northeastern Connecticut to advocate for greater equity in
state funding for rural schools, including the calculation of Education
Cost Sharing (ECS) funds. The group also explores opportunities for
neighboring communities to collaborate and share services and programs.
These conversations matter as districts work together to meet students’
and communities’ needs.

- Designated High School Discussion: The board received a comprehensive
presentation from members of Woodstock Academy’s leadership team
regarding the programs and opportunities available to students. During
the public comment portion of the meeting, the board also heard
community members’ perspectives on designating a high school for
Eastford students. The leadership team from E.O. Smith High School will
provide a similar presentation at the Nov. 12 Board of Education
meeting.

- The board also heard an overview from Terry Cote, a member of the
board’s Ad Hoc Committee, regarding the Community Conversation held on
Sept. 24. More than 100 people attended this event, demonstrating the
community’s interest in this important discussion. Special thanks to
Nick Caruso from the Connecticut Association of Boards of Education
(CABE) for facilitating the conversation.

The board continues its research and discussion as it considers the
designated high school for Eastford students. A designated high school
is the school for which the board provides tuition and transportation
costs. Woodstock Academy is Eastford’s current designated high school.

We recognize the importance of this decision and the value of continued
community input as the board considers the opportunities available to
our students.

- Board Committee Updates: In addition to its full board meeting, two
board subcommittees met during October:

- Curriculum Committee: On Oct. 8, the committee reviewed new book
selections recommended by the principal and the Grade 4 teacher. The
committee brought the recommendation to the full board for further
consideration on Oct. 8, and it was approved unanimously.

- Policy Committee: On Oct. 13, the committee reviewed several proposed
policies that will be brought forward to the board for further
discussion and action.

- Looking Ahead: The 2027–2028 Budget — The board is also beginning to
look ahead to the budget planning process for the 2027-2028 school year.
These conversations are important because the decisions we make today
help shape the opportunities and experiences available to our students
in the years ahead.

Community input is an important part of this process. The Dec. 10 Board
of Education meeting will include a special public comment opportunity
for community members to share their thoughts and priorities regarding
the upcoming budget. I encourage you to attend and participate. Your
perspectives matter, and we value the opportunity to hear from the
people we serve.

- In Gratitude: As Coretta Scott King reminded us, “The greatness of a
community is most accurately measured by the compassionate actions of
its members.” As we enter this season of Thanksgiving, I sincerely thank
our students, staff, families, and community members for all you do to
help our students learn, grow, and thrive. Eastford is fortunate to be
part of such a caring and giving community. We are also grateful for our
town leadership’s continued support and the strong partnerships that
help sustain our schools. Thank you for being an important part of the
Eastford Elementary School community. I wish you and your families a
happy, healthy, and peaceful Thanksgiving.
