---
title: From the First Selectman
author: Deb Richards
---

Help shape the future of Eastford! Eastford is fortunate to have
dedicated, knowledgeable residents who volunteer their time to serve on
our commissions and committees. These volunteers play an important role
in helping our town thrive. Over the past 10 years, fewer residents have
been willing to serve, as longtime volunteers have retired or moved
away. We need new volunteers, fresh ideas, and new perspectives to keep
Eastford moving forward. There are many ways you can get involved and
make a difference:

- Planning Commission: Help guide Eastford’s long-term growth, land use,
and development.

- Conservation & Historic Preservation Commission: This non-regulatory
town board works to protect Eastford’s natural, agricultural, historic,
and archaeological resources through education, information, and
conservation projects.

- Inland Wetlands & Watercourses Commission: Helps review and regulate
activities in or near wetlands and watercourses, address land-use
matters as required by Connecticut General Statutes §22a-45, and protect
these important natural resources.

- Recreation Commission: Do you enjoy the wonderful programs and
activities offered by the Recreation Commission? Consider joining the
commission or volunteering to help with recreation events and programs.

Other opportunities to serve include:

- Municipal Agent for the Elderly: Every town and city in Connecticut is
required to have a Municipal Agent for Aging. This person helps
residents age 60 and older by providing information about local
services, programs, and benefits.

- Emergency Management Director: The Emergency Management Director plays
a vital role in preparing Eastford for emergencies and disasters.
Responsibilities include planning for emergencies, coordinating response
and recovery efforts, working with other agencies, supporting Emergency
Operations Center activities, and ensuring compliance with state and
federal requirements. If you are interested in learning more about this
important opportunity, please contact the Selectmen’s Office.

If you are interested in helping in any of these roles, contact the
Selectmen’s Office at emily.john@eastfordct.gov.

Your voice matters! Eastford needs new ideas and perspectives —
including those of our younger residents and families. Whether you have
lived here for generations or are newer to town, your experience, ideas,
and energy can help shape the community where you live, work, and raise
your family. You can make a difference in Eastford.

Help shape the future of your community. Volunteer. Join a committee.
Get involved.
