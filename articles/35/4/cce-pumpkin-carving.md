---

EASTFORD — The Congregational Church of Eastford hosted a free pumpkin
carving event on Sunday, Oct. 18, at Eastford Elementary School.
Stations were set up for people of all ages to carve and decorate
pumpkins for Halloween, along with free refreshments. Pictured, Emerson
Labonte focuses hard on his pumpkin piece of art. *Courtesy photo*
