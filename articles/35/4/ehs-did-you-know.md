Did you know that in 1951, a runoff election was needed to determine
Eastford’s third selectman?

In the election held on Oct. 1, 1951, Harold Carpenter (D) and Robert
MacFarlane (D) each received 13 votes. The run-off was held on Oct. 8,
and Carpenter won by a vote of 110-6.

![sealed ballot box](/assets/images/35-4-voting-box-1951.jpg)

This election marked the last time the voting box (pictured with this
article) was used in Eastford elections. It still contains the paper
ballots cast that day and is sealed with a white strip of paper signed
by Registrars Henry A. Lawton and Alfred E. Warren, and Moderator John
H. Smith. The box was donated to the Historical Society by Kris Karlsen.
He purchased it at auction when the town moved its office from the Ivy
Glenn basement to the then-new Town Office Building in 1971.
