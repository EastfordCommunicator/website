title: Eastford Food Pantry Update
author: Audrey Carabeau

The Eastford Food Pantry is located at the Eastford Baptist Church, 133
Union Rd. Please see our hours of operation below. If you reside in
Eastford and are in need of food assistance, call Audrey Carabeau at
(860) 933-7685 to sign up. We have the resources to help you! All
referrals are kept confidential. Delivery is available to those who are
homebound.

Thank you to everyone who donated food, home goods, and money in
October. Your donations are greatly appreciated by our pantry families.
The Eastford Food Pantry relies on community donations. All donations to
the pantry can be dropped off at Bowen’s Garage or at the Town Office
Building during regular business hours. Check donations can be mailed
to: Eastford Food Pantry, 16 Westford Rd., Eastford, CT 06242.

Donation suggestions include hearty soups, rice mixes, healthy snacks,
deodorant, and laundry detergent. Thank you for your support!

Eastford Food Pantry hours for November are Monday, Nov. 2, 8-10:30
a.m., and Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2-4 p.m. Thanksgiving turkey distribution
date to be announced.
