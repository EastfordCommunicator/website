title: From the Eastford Grove Cemetery Association
---

From the Board of Trustees:

- Flags: American flags and fire department flags will be removed on
Thursday, November 12th. Plot owners who remove their military flag
holders for the winter are reminded to reset them the week prior to
Memorial Day.

- Trustees’ Meeting: Tuesday, Nov. 17, 7 p.m., Ivy Glenn

- Seasonal Decorations: Mowing and turf maintenance continues well into
the month of November. If you wish to place seasonal/artificial
decorations (i.e., wreaths, logs, or baskets) on your plot, please wait
until Thanksgiving, Nov. 26. After March 15, 2027, the decorations will
be removed for the start of the mowing season.

- Perpetual Care Reminder: Flowers that are planted at the base of a
monument must be within 12” of the base. If plants are not maintained
and grow beyond that limit, they will be cut back.

- Plot/Space Purchase: Appointments can be made to view the cemetery
grounds and to select a plot for purchase. To make an appointment, call
(860) 428-0280.

*Board of Trustees: Alex Kowalchuk, Carolyn Hazard, David Barlow, Holly
Singleton, Ken Grube, Lauren Anderson, Linda Torgeson, Rick Torgeson*
