---
title: From the Grove Cemetery Association Board of Trustees
---

The Eastford Grove Cemetery Association is committed to helping families
with their decisions regarding plot purchase and plot size as they
relate to burial wishes. It is with this in mind that the Association is
offering its assistance to you, whether you are making plans for
yourself or for your loved one. The decisions you may face are more
complex than most people realize and are usually not easy to make when
people are grieving. Our board can confidentially aid you in considering
various plot options for full and cremation burials, memorial (flush)
markers and monuments. Feel free to call (860) 428-0280 to schedule an
appointment.

The EGCA is a non-profit organization.
