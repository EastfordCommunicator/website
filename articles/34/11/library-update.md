author: Katie Ball

Our much-anticipated Summer Reading Program this year will be
celebrating America250. We will immerse our patrons in the lives and
times of the colonists. Thomas Angels, a colonial reenactor from History
Without Walls, will kick off our program on July 7 at 3 p.m. with a
presentation for all ages. Children and adults alike are invited to
participate in our six-week Summer Reading Program, which will feature a
different theme each week. From colonial life, arts and fare, to
colonial entertainment, gardening, toys and games, we will travel back
in time to 1776. Please inquire at our front desk if you have any
questions.

Library hours are Tuesdays, 11 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.; Wednesdays, 10 a.m. to
5 p.m.; Thursdays, 11 a.m. to 6:30 p.m.; and Saturdays, 9 a.m. to 12
p.m.
