---
title: Recreation Commission Update
---

Thank you so much for your participation in the Heart of Eastford 5K Run
and Experience Eastford. As we move into a new fiscal year, we are
making some leadership changes and looking to grow our team. There are a
lot of good ideas out there to strengthen our town's activities for
kids, adult sports, and activities for adults, like bus trips, concerts,
and special classes. Right now, our team is very small, but we would
like to expand to better serve our terrific community. Maybe you might
join the team? [Contact us on the town website here](https://www.eastfordct.gov/recreation-commission).
