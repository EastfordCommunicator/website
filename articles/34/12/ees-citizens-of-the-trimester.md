---
title: Citizens of the Trimester
---

EASTFORD --- On Thursday, June 11, Eastford Elementary School
sixth-grader Owen Barlow, left, and fourth-grader Hope McGurl, were
honored at a meeting of the Eastford Board of Education for being
recently named the Citizens of the Trimester. The honor is given in
recognition of leadership qualities, respect for others and property,
encouragement and support of peers, and involvement in school
activities. Their nominations were read to the board, and each was
awarded a certificate.
