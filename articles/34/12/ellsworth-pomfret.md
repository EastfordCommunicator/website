---
title: Ellsworth Graduates from Pomfret School
---

POMFRET --- Rob Ellsworth, of Eastford, graduated from Pomfret School on
Sunday, May 24. He earned a place on the High Honor Roll in all 12
trimesters while serving as a two-sport varsity captain. Rob will attend
Wesleyan University in the fall as a committed member of the Wesleyan
Cardinals baseball team.
