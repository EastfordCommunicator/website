---
title: Boyd, Gordon Field Questions at Legislative Update Event
---



EASTFORD --- State Sen. Jeff Gordon and State Rep. Pat Boyd held a
Legislative Update coffeehouse hour at Always Moore on Saturday, April 18. Both Gordon and Boyd highlighted their bipartisan efforts in the
northeast corner, stating that they may not always agree, but they like
to work together for their constituents. Eleven people in attendance
asked questions ranging from the topics of budgets and town aid, to
special education and school costs, to the affordable housing bill, the
cost of solar permitting and plug-in solar, and changes to the
vaccination laws. Gordon shared that there is a lot going on right now
in Hartford with some bills being redrafted daily. However, Boyd stated
that the session ends May 6, no matter what. Connecticut must pass a
balanced budget or there can be a civil suit. Residents can go to
www.cga.ct.gov to see bills, testimony, what committees are
doing, and more. Check in regularly as the page is updated frequently.
