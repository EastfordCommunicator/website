---
title: EES Students Awarded AHA Certificates
---

EASTFORD --- It is with pleasure for American Heart Association
Instructor Jeannine Spink to announce that grades 6, 7, and 8 Health
class students have successfully completed three months of training in
the skills of CPR/Defibrillation, Heimlich Maneuver, Rescue Breathing
and Basic First Aid for infants, children, and adults in accordance with
the standards and guidelines established by the American Heart
Association.

Beginning in January, each class met weekly during their Health class
blocks until they successfully completed all the elements required and
awarded a Certificate of Completion and Understanding.

Thank you to Principal Carole McCombe for allowing these students to
enhance their learning process and build confidence in knowing how to
recognize and render basic care until help arrives. Eastford parents
need to be proud of their students as participants in Eastford's
HeartSafe Community Program.
