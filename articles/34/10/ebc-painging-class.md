---
title: EBC Painting Class Delivers the Joy of Painting
---


EASTFORD --- To celebrate the upcoming Easter season, Eastford Baptist
Church (EBC) hosted a Community Painting Class on Friday, March 27. More
than 20 people came out to enjoy a relaxed time of painting, which
depicted a series of three crosses and an empty tomb to symbolize the
death and resurrection of Jesus Christ. EBC regularly hosts Community
Painting Classes. For more information, visit www.eastfordbaptist.com,
and check the "Events" tab.
