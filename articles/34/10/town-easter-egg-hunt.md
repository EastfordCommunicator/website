---
title: Community Turns out for Egg-Cellent Easter Fun
---

EASTFORD --- The town's Easter tradition continued on Saturday, March
28, as the Recreation Commission hosted its annual Easter Egg Hunt at
Eastford Elementary School. Dozens of children gathered in surprisingly
cold temperatures to hunt for eggs and enjoy some time outside looking
for treats.
