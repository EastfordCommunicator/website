---
title: Library Trivia Night
---

EASTFORD --- On Thursday, April 2, the Eastford Public Library hosted an
Adult Trivia Night, as host Jeremy Barlow tested the knowledge of
participants. From numbers to flags of the world, sound clips of famous
voices to chemistry knowledge and more, Jeremy posed some challenging
questions to the teams. A great time was had by all.
