## May

### Tuesday, May 5

Eastford Public Library will host "Homeschool STEAM Hour" at 10:30 a.m.
Please register, as space is limited. The library will close at 5 p.m.
Sorry for the inconvenience.

### Thursday, May 7

The National Day of Prayer event will take place on Thursday, May 7, at
7 p.m. at the Eastford School flagpole. Visit nationaldayofprayer.org
for more information.

Eastford Public Library will host "Storytime" at 11 a.m. Join us for a
story, craft, snack and time of free play. In the afternoon, we will
host a Dungeons & Dragons club from 4:30 to 5:30 p.m. (ages 11-13). This
group will continue to meet every Thursday. Please register.

### Saturday, May 9

Eastford Public Library will host a "Mother's Day Craft" at 10:30 a.m.
Please register. In the afternoon, The Page Turners, will meet at 1 p.m.
to discuss "In the Garden of Beasts" by Erik Larson. Copies of the book
will be on display and available for check out.

Have someone special in your life --- like a mother, father, aunt,
uncle, grandparent, or family friend? Help send our seventh and eighth
graders to Washington D.C. this May in a special way! Eastford
Elementary School will host a "Someone Special Dance," Saturday, May 9,
from 3:30 to 5:30 p.m. at the EES Gym. Tickets are \$10 per "couple."
Additional family members can attend for \$3 per ticket. There will be a
bake sale, and a photo booth. All proceeds will benefit the annual
eighth grade Washington D.C. field trip.

### Thursday, May 14

### Saturday, May 16

On Saturday, May 16, Eastford Baptist Church will host its 4th Annual
Olympians LEGO Derby! The event, which is for kids in Grades 1-6, starts
at 1:30 p.m. at the church's Family Activity Center gym, where kids will
build their LEGO racecars. Racing heats begin at 2 p.m. The event will
also feature snacks, crafts, games, and other activities! Parental
pickup at 4 p.m. Join us for a great afternoon of racing action! For
more information, e-mail <ebcolympians@gmail.com>!

### Monday, May 25

Eastford honors America's 250th anniversary! Join us Monday, May 25, for
the Town of Eastford's annual Memorial Day Ceremony and Parade. Memorial
Services/Military Salutes: General Lyon Cemetery, 8:30 a.m.; Grove
Cemetery, 9 a.m. Memorial Day Parade starts at 9:30 a.m. from Eastford
Elementary School. Community members are invited to participate!
Ceremony will immediately follow the parade at the Veterans Memorial,
featuring the laying of a memorial wreath and a military salute, the
recognition of an Eastford veteran, and honored speaker Mark W. Sheldon
Sr., 1st VP of the CT Society of the Sons of the American Revolution.
All are welcome to attend!

### Wednesday, May 27

Eastford Public Library will host a special Bridgerton prelude. Ashley
Baxter from Movement Magic in Putnam will offer a one-hour group Regency
Dance Class from 6 to 7 p.m. Come learn some steps in advance of our
Bridgerton Soiree on Saturday, May 30. Following the class, we will make
some hair accessories such as headbands and fascinators to wear to the
event. Please register.

### Thursday, May 28

### Saturday, May 30th

Dearest Gentle Reader, Eastford Public Library cordially invites you to
our Bridgerton Soiree at 6 p.m. Whether you love the novels by Julia
Quinn or have been swept away by the Netflix series, this event is sure
