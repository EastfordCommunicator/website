---
title: From the First Selectman
author: Deb Richards
---

Volunteers are ordinary people with extraordinary hearts. They give the
gift of their time to inspire, build, grow, teach, and guide others.
They ask for nothing in return, yet the value of their contributions is
immeasurable. Volunteers extend a hand, give a hand, and create lasting
change that touches lives for years to come.

Thank you to all our dedicated volunteers, including the Planning
Commission, Conservation and Historic Preservation Committee, Crystal
Pond Park Commission, Transfer Station Committee, Emergency Management,
Agent for the Elderly, Food Pantry Coordinators, Municipal Veteran
Representative, Recreation Commission, Eastford Public Library Board of
Trustees, Board of Assessment Appeals, Inland Wetlands Committee, and
Bowen's Field Committee.

Each of you makes a meaningful difference in our town. We are deeply
grateful for your commitment and service --- this community would not be
the same without you.
