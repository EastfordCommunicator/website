---
title: From the Grove Cemetery Association Board of Trustees
---

- Memorial Day Weekend: American flags and Fire Department flags will be
placed at eligible gravesites on Friday, May 22, at 5 p.m. The placement
of any flag, including the American flag, is limited to those flags
placed on the graves of military and fire department veterans by the
Board of Trustees. Unauthorized flags will be removed.

- Flag Holder: If you are interested in ordering a flag holder for your
veteran's flag, call (860) 428-0280 by May 1.

- Plot/Space Purchase: Appointments can be made to view the cemetery
grounds and to select a plot for purchase. Call (860) 428-0280 for more
information.

- Plot Diagrams Available: Plot owners, or a family member, may obtain a
diagram of their plot by writing to: EGCA Inc., P.O. Box 284, Eastford,
CT 06242.

- Mowing Season is March 15 to Thanksgiving: Only live flowers may be
planted, or placed in plastic pots, within 12 inches of the base of a
monument; all artificial flowers and ornaments are prohibited and will
be removed.

For more information, contact a member of the Board of Trustees: Linda
Torgeson, Rick Torgeson, Lauren Anderson, Ken Grube, David Barlow,
Carolyn Hazard, Alex Kowalchuk.
