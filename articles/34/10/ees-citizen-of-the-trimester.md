---
title: Citizen of the Trimester
---

EASTFORD --- At its meeting on Thursday, April 9, the Eastford Board of
Education recognized third grader Savannah DeLay as Citizen of the
Trimester. Savannah was selected for this honor by faculty in
recognition of her leadership qualities, respect for others and
property, encouragement and support of peers, and involvement in school
activities. DeLay was presented with a certificate the April 9 meeting
by Principal Carole McCombe, while her parents, Travis and Becca DeLay,
looked with pride.
