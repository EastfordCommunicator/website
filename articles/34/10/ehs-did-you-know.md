--
title: Did You Know?
author: Eastford Historical Society
---

Did you know that during the horse and buggy days, when many mail trains
were running, it was possible to send a letter to Hartford and get a
reply the same day? The letter would leave Eastford at 4 a.m. via Putnam
and the reply would come via North Windham and be delivered to Eastford
at 7 p.m. the same day. Oh, for the good old days!

[To learn more about the Historical Society and Eastford history visit us
