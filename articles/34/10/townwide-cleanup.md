---
title: "Spring Cleaning: Town Roads Get Facelift Thanks to Clean-Up Event"
---

EASTFORD --- More than 30 volunteers gathered on Sunday, April 12, to
clean up the streets of Eastford, traversing nearly 17 combined miles of
roadway to remove trash, in an effort to beautify the town. Volunteers
met at the Eastford Elementary School and Town Office Building parking
lot at 12:30 p.m. for a hot dog lunch, followed by road work, which
ended at around 3 p.m., where volunteers returned to the parking lot for
ice cream. The event was jointly spearheaded by Eastford Baptist Church
and the Congregational Church of Eastford.
