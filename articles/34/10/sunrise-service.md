---
title: A Day of Celebration
---

EASTFORD --- Despite cloud cover and precipitation blocking the town's
view of the rising sun on Sunday, April 5, both Eastford Baptist Church
and the Congregational Church of Eastford celebrated Resurrection Sunday
with a Sunrise Service. Dozens turned out at 6:30 a.m., both at Buell's
Orchard (EBC) and at 8 Church Rd. (the former site of CCE), to sing
songs and hear the message of the risen Savior.
