title: PELL Spring Season in Full Swing

POMFRET/EASTFORD --- The Pomfret Eastford Little League (PELL) spring
season has begun! In all, 148 children registered this year. Opening Day
was held Saturday, April 18.

This season, PELL is featuring the following teams: 1 Majors baseball; 1
AAA baseball; 2 AA baseball; 2 Minors Softball; 3 Coach Pitch Baseball;
1 Coach Pitch Softball; 4 Tee Ball.

Field clean-up days were a huge success! In Eastford, clean-up was held
at Bowen Memorial Field on Saturday, April 4. In Pomfret, clean-up was
held on Saturday, April 11.

The PELL Midseason Classic will take place on Saturday, May 9, at 63
Hampton Rd., in Pomfret. The day will feature a full schedule of games
starting at 9 a.m., ending with a Majors night game at 7:30 p.m. Yummys
food truck and Frozen Cheeks Frosty Treats will be there. Jump Zone is
donating a dunk tank and games.
