---
title: Owen King Visits Eastford for 'Author Talk' Event at Library
---


EASTFORD --- On Saturday, April 25, Eastford Public Library hosted an
"Author Talk" with writer, television and film producer Owen King. The
event was filled to capacity. King, who is also the son of author
Stephen King, sat down with Jody Blanchette to speak about his passion
for writing screen plays, comics and books, his creative process, and
how he enjoys collaborating with others on projects. He also spoke about
books that inspired him growing up, read to him by his parents; his
favorite landscaping job as a teen; his adoration of cats; and why he
didn't care much for Halloween as a child, and more. After a final
series of rapid-fire questions by Jody, there was time for questions
from the audience. He then autographed books and paused for photos,
while guests were treated to a themed reception.
