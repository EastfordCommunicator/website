Did you know? A dog's sense of smell is 10,000 to 100,000 times more
acute than ours. To put that in perspective, if we can smell a teaspoon
of sugar in a cup of coffee, a dog could smell that same teaspoon in two
Olympic-sized swimming pools!

## Dog of the Month

Meet Ruby! An adorable 30-ish pound mystery mix who looks like she could
be some sort of basenji mix, but only a DNA test will reveal the truth.
Ruby did an amazing job raising her seven puppies, and now it's her turn
to find a forever home. She is a true cuddle bug who will happily settle
right into your lap. Ruby gets along well with other dogs, has done
great with the foster's cat, and loves children. She's learning to walk
on a leash, is almost a pro at pottying outside, and rides great in the
car. She is under 2 years old, medically up to date, microchipped and
spayed.
