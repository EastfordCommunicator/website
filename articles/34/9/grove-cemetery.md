title: From the Grove Cemetery Association Board of Trustees
---

- Mowing Season --- March 15 to Thanksgiving: Artificial flowers,
ornaments, and other objects are not allowed during the mowing season.
However, live flowers may be planted or placed in plastic pots within
12" of the base of the monument. It is not permissible to plant flowers
or place potted plants near flush markers.

- Board of Corporators Annual Meeting: Wednesday, April 15, 7 p.m. at
the Town Office Building.

- There is one vacancy on the Board of Corporators. If interested, call
(860) 428-0280.
