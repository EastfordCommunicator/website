Photo Kicker:

Calvin's tempest

CUTLINE: *Courtesy photos*

EASTFORD --- In the second part of a vicious one-two punch, Blizzard
Calvin rampaged through the area on Sunday, Feb. 22 and Monday, Feb. 23,
leaving more than a foot of wet, heavy snow in its wake, and leading to
three consecutive snow days at Eastford Elementary School. It was a
massive clean-up effort after Winter Storm Benjamin had already dumped
18 inches on Eastford less than a month before. Pictured above, Liv,
Anna, and Grace Moore spend some quality time outside enjoying their
snow day. At left, young Charli enjoys her first snowfall. Her mother
Christa wrote: "Such a beautiful sight."
