HEAD:

EBC Chili Cookoff features spicy spoons full of fellowship and fun

CUTLINES: *Courtesy photos*

EASTFORD --- Eastford Baptist Church hosted its annual Chili Cookoff
Sunday, March 1, after the morning's worship service. Eight chili
recipes were sampled by three non-partial judges (Kris Kurak, Mike
Norman, and Kevin Rumrill) to determine the top three chilis. The
winners were --- Third Place: Emma & Amelia; Second Place: Kerri Garry;
First Place: Steve Rempel.
